# Yours Truly

A World of Warcraft addon for Classic Era and World of Warcraft: Forever:
letters from the people you helped.

- **The letters.** Finish a quest chain that changed someone's life, and
  they write to you: Iverron from the cave you found him by, Maybell Maclure
  after her secret visit to the river, Gryan Stoutmantle once VanCleef is
  dead, Gazlowe, who still owes you. Each in their own voice, about what
  happened and what came after.
- **The mailbox.** The letter spends a little while in the post, then waits
  at the next mailbox you open, sealed, beside the game's mail window. Click
  to break the seal. Nothing tells you it's coming.
- **The book.** Every letter you receive, kept: who wrote, from where, the
  chain it answers. A few achievements along the way.

English and French. `/yourstruly` opens the book.

The game's data comes from [wow-data](https://github.com/salty-max/wow-data):
the game clients' tables, [Questie](https://github.com/Questie/Questie)'s
database and the [cmangos](https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db) Classic
database. The letters are ours.
