# Gryan Stoutmantle

**Who.** Gryan Stoutmantle (234), who heads the People's Militia from the stone tower on Sentinel Hill. The anchor is The Defias Brotherhood (166): Edwin VanCleef killed in his hideout, his head brought back. The chain before it: Wiley in Lakeshire, who owed Gryan a favour, Mathias Shaw of SI:7, the Stonemasons' Guild that rebuilt Stormwind under VanCleef, the Defias Traitor who led the way to the hideout. Gryan knew VanCleef's name "well", was unnerved that "a man so industrious and talented" turned scoundrel, and was saddened to sentence any man to death.

**The voice.** A militia captain who writes the way he gives orders: plainly, in short paragraphs, no flourish. Decent and responsible for the people still there. The chain cost him something: he asked for a man's death and knew what that man had built. He will not give a promise just because someone needs to hear one. His thanks are straightforward.

**Never.** Triumph, gloating over the dead, a promise the plains can't keep.

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** VanCleef killed (166), the builder he was and Gryan knowing his name (141, Shaw's report and Gryan's answer), Gryan's sorrow at ordering a death (166's own words), Farmer Furlbrow (237) and his family driven from their farm (36, 64), the Defias still on the roads. **Ours.** The walls, Furlbrow's wish for a date, the quiet watch he doesn't trust yet. No farm is said to be safe, no road safer.

## Letter

$N,

I knew Edwin VanCleef's name long before I knew his crimes. There are walls in Stormwind he raised with his own hands. I am glad it is done, and I did not enjoy asking it of you.

Furlbrow wants to take his family home. I had to tell him not yet. He wanted a day he could mark down, and I could not give him one. We still need men on the roads.

The night watch passed without an alarm. I do not trust it yet, and I have told the men not to.

You have my thanks, and the militia's. I wanted you to hear it from me.

Gryan Stoutmantle
The People's Militia
