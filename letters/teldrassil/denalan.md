# Denalan

**Who.** Denalan (2080), a night elf botanist camped at the eastern end of Lake Al'Ameth, "studying and experimenting on the plant life of Teldrassil". The player carried his late package of Rare Earth from Dolanaar, sent by his colleague Syral Bladeleaf (997), then gathered Timberling Seeds, which he meant to plant in special soil, hoping they would sprout more docile, because "it is disturbing to see how angry the timberlings have become... I believe it has something to do with the soil" (918). Then the player found a Glowing Fruit on a Strange Fruited Plant and brought it to him (930, the anchor): "this fruit is exotic. Perhaps its seeds were brought here from far off... it seems to have reacted very strangely with the soil of Teldrassil." He ended the talk to study it.

**The voice.** A scholar's: courteous, careful, every claim hedged ("I believe", "it seems", "perhaps"), and openly delighted by what he doesn't understand. Writes the way he keeps notes, sorting what he has observed from what he hasn't. Worry for the tree sits under the curiosity, and shows once.

**Never.** A finding (what troubles the soil is for later chains), the seeds' outcome (the sprouts of 919 are another quest), the fruit's origin settled, the game's own "as far as Azeroth" (it reads as a slip), an errand.

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** The rare earth from Dolanaar (997), the seeds and the special soil, the angry timberlings and his worry about the soil (918), the fruit found under the plant's fronds, exotic, perhaps from far off, reacting strangely with Teldrassil's soil (930). **Ours.** The fruit kept wrapped and away from the soil, his preference that the fault be the fruit's, the watched seed (a translator uses the language's own watched-pot saying), his tally of what he knows, the rare earth gone into the special soil, the player's eye, lent to him.

## Letter

$N,

The fruit you found is the most interesting thing in my camp. I keep it wrapped, well away from the soil, until I understand what passed between them. Was the fault in the fruit, or in the earth of Teldrassil? I would rather it were the fruit.

The timberling seeds are in the special soil, with the rare earth you carried from Dolanaar, and I look in on them more often than is useful. I am told a watched seed never grows. I have found that neither true nor false.

What I know: the plant that bore the fruit is a stranger here. Perhaps its seed was carried from very far off. What I do not know: nearly everything else. It is a wonderful position to be in.

You have a good eye, $N. Thank you for lending it to me.

Respectfully,
Denalan
