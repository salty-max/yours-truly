# Iverron

**Who.** Iverron (8584), a young night elf of Shadowglen. Bitten by Githyiss the Vile (1994) while exploring the spider cave north of the glade, he sent the player to Dirania Silvershine (8583) (3519), who made an antidote from Hyacinth Mushrooms, Moonpetal Lilies and Webwood Ichor (3521). It stayed good for five minutes, and the player got it to him in time (3522, the anchor). Before all that, Dirania had worried because he visits her at the same time every day and hadn't come (4495). The game leaves him sitting by the cave: "I'm always getting into trouble and Dirania, well, she always manages to help me out of it... I think I will sit here for a while longer... Well, that, and I was hoping you'd clear a path for me through those vicious Webwood spiders." The first letter a night elf gets.

**The voice.** Young, friendly, sheepish, never short of words. Owns up to his scrapes before anyone can point them out, and backs up with a "well" when the truth turns out longer than the sentence. Fond of Dirania, more than he says. A friend's letter, light, from where the game left him.

**Never.** Bravado, a real danger (it's the first hour), the spiders or Githyiss gone (they come back), a path he asks the player to clear, Githyiss killed by the player (the chain doesn't ask it).

**French.** Tutoie: a friend's letter to the one who saved him.

**The game's.** The bite and Githyiss the Vile (3519), Dirania and the antidote's five minutes (3522), the daily visits (4495), "always getting into trouble" and Dirania getting him out of it, the spiders between him and the glade, his sitting by the cave (3522). **Ours.** Dirania's messages and her warnings repeated, her preferring to know where he is, the spiders looking busy, the greeting from the cave. Nobody walks anywhere new: he is where the game keeps him.

## Letter

Hello from the cave, $N!

I'm still right where you found me. I feel completely better, I promise. Well, mostly. There are still a great many Webwood spiders between me and the rest of Shadowglen, and a sensible elf waits until they look busy.

Dirania sends word that I'm fit to walk home. She also reminds me, each time, that she always warned me about those spiders. Well, she did.

I used to visit her at the same time every day. Now she knows exactly where I am at every hour, and I think she prefers it.

I'm always getting into trouble, and somebody always gets me out of it. Usually it's Dirania. This time it was you, with five minutes to do it in. Thank you.

Your friend,
Iverron
