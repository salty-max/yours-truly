# Prospector Remtravel

**Who.** Prospector Remtravel (2917) of the Explorers' League, a dwarf, at his excavation in southern Darkshore. The chain (729 to 943, The Absent Minded Prospector): Archaeologist Hollee (2913) ran for help when golems overran the dig, "But the prospector is rather... um... oblivious ...to his surroundings." The player finds him still chiselling ("Hand me my stone brush... Hey, you're not Hollee!"), escorts him out (731), and carries his mysterious fossil to Chief Archaeologist Greywhisker in Darnassus (741), then to Archaeologist Flagongut in Menethil Harbor (942), who wants his own Stone of Relu back from the raptors that swallowed it (943, the anchor: "Now to see if I can use the stone to decipher the powers of the fossils").

**The voice.** Absent-minded, cheerful, digressive: forgets names, loses tools, notices sediment instead of golems, and is entirely sincere. Writes as he talks, losing the thread and finding it again. Fond of Hollee, who handles everything.

**Never.** An errand (he looks for his brush, he doesn't ask the player to), what Flagongut learns from the fossils (later), the golems said gone, a cruel joke at his expense.

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** The dig, the golems he didn't notice, Hollee, the stone brush, the escort, the fossil's road to Darnassus and Menethil, Flagongut and his stone (729 to 943). **Ours.** Learning from Hollee what happened, the stroll, the sediment, hoping Flagongut writes to him and not to Hollee, the brush looked for while writing.

## Letter

$N,

Hollee tells me that you are the one who walked me out of the dig, and that I was not, as I believed at the time, simply taking a stroll. She says there were golems. I recall a very interesting layer of sediment.

She also tells me my fossil has been to Darnassus and then to Menethil Harbor, which is more travel than I have done in years, and that a fellow named Flagongut has it now, along with some stone of his own. Splendid! I hope he writes. I hope he writes to me, that is, and not to Hollee, though Hollee does seem to receive everything.

I have been looking for my stone brush. I mention it only because I have been looking for it the whole time I was writing this, and it seemed rude not to say so.

With thanks, and I think apologies,
Prospector Remtravel
