# Magistrate Solomon

**Who.** Magistrate Solomon (344), who governs Lakeshire in the Redridge Mountains. The chain (120 to 146, the Messenger quests): Lakeshire "under constant siege", he sends the player with letters for help, to General Marcus Jonathan in Stormwind (reinforcements "as soon as word is given from His Majesty"), to Gryan Stoutmantle in Westfall, who has none to spare ("my heart is heavy with the loss of good men"), and to Lord Ello Ebonlocke in Darkshire, who can't send his Night Watch past the forest. Between: "Something is amiss in our Kingdom. I fear this is the beginning of conflict, not the end", and "Bah, I should not ponder aloud." The anchor (146): "By the Light! The Stormwind Army retreated from Westfall. And the Guard no longer protects Darkshire? There is some treachery abound... Something is amiss in Stormwind and I must get to the bottom of it."

**The voice.** A judge who answers every trouble with a letter: formal, measured, fair to every side, and alarmed under the measure. Catches himself thinking aloud and scolds himself for it ("Bah"). The one writer who has always sent the player somewhere, and this time doesn't.

**Never.** Lady Prestor or any wink at who is behind it (the plan's rule: a letter that reads the same after the reveal), an errand or a summons, Lakeshire said safe (it is still besieged), a word against the King.

**French.** Vouvoie. On Forever, Lakeshire is "Comté-du-Lac" and Darkshire "Sombre-Comté", Classic Era's names swapped in there (classic-names.json).

**The game's.** The siege, the three letters and their answers (Stormwind's promise, Westfall's dead, Darkshire's Night Watch), treachery, something amiss in Stormwind, getting to the bottom of it, not pondering aloud (120 to 146). **Ours.** A letter to carry nowhere, the replies reread, reasonable answers that make no sense together, his finding whose treachery it is, the truth even when it was no.

## Letter

$N,

For once, a letter from me that you need not carry anywhere.

I have read the three replies you brought me more often than a magistrate ought to read anything. Stormwind will send reinforcements when the King gives the word. Westfall has no men to spare and its own dead to bury. Darkshire cannot send its Night Watch past the edge of its own forest. Each answer is reasonable. Together they make no sense at all.

Lakeshire holds, for now. There is treachery somewhere in all this, and I mean to learn whose. Bah. I should not ponder aloud, not even on paper.

You walked a long way for a township that owed you nothing, and each time you came back with the truth, even when the truth was no. Thank you.

Magistrate Solomon
Lakeshire
