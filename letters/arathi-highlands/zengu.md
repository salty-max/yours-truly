# Zengu

**Who.** Zengu (2703), a Darkspear troll in Hammerfall, in the Arathi Highlands. The chain (639 to 646): "In ancient times, trolls fought against the Arathorian Empire and our most capable adversary, Lord Ignaeus of Strom, named Trollbane. He rode into battle wielding Trol'kalar--troll slayer, in their ancient tongue" (639); the sigils of Strom, Thoradin (rebuilt by Tor'gan), Arathor (Lieutenant Valorcall) and Trollbane (Prince Galen), "The blood of Trollbane will no longer haunt my people... Soon, very soon, we will strike against the hated trolls of Zul'Gurub" (640 to 644); the tomb opened, the sword freed from the stone hands (645). The anchor (646): "For generations, the forces of Strom and Arathor ravaged the trolls. Now their greatest weapon is in our hands. Our inching progress in the steamy forests of Zul'Gurub will be no more. Your assistance has been great, $N, and the Darkspear trolls are indebted to you. I will be sure that the sword is delivered to Nimboya at Grom'gol."

**The voice.** A proud Darkspear with a long memory: "mon", history told like a grudge settled, awe at holding the sword. Exultant, not cruel.

**Never.** Galen or Stromgarde's defenders gone for good (they come back), the sword's use at Zul'Gurub told (later), Nimboya's own story (another chain), Gazlowe's debt ("indebted" becomes remembering who carries burdens).

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** Trollbane and Trol'kalar, troll slayer, generations of Strom and Arathor against the trolls, the sigils, the tomb, the sword in troll hands, Zul'Gurub's steamy forests, the sword sent to Nimboya at Grom'gol (639 to 646). **Ours.** Three cloths and two carriers, the grandfathers who ran from it, his hands shaking, whether the sword remembers whose side it was made for, in their hands by the player's doing.

## Letter

$N,

Trol'kalar is on its way to Nimboya at Grom'gol, wrapped in three cloths and carried by two of our fastest, because neither would let the other carry it alone.

You know what the name means, mon. Troll slayer. For generations the men of Strom and Arathor carried it against my people, and our grandfathers' grandfathers learned to run at the sight of it. Now a troll has held it. I have held it. My hands shook, and I am not ashamed to say so.

It will be wanted where our people fight, in the steamy forests of Zul'Gurub. Whether it remembers whose side it was made for, I cannot say.

That it is in our hands at all is your doing, $N.

Zengu
Hammerfall
