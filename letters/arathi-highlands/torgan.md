# Tor'gan

**Who.** Tor'gan (2706), a troll shaman in Hammerfall, in the Arathi Highlands. The chain (655 to 847): Gor'mul, who saw Doomhammer fall, "That the blow of a coward would strike down our greatest hero... What hope was there? What hope is there..." (655); Tor'gan: "Gor'mul was a captive here when Thrall and Orgrim fought to free the camp, but as the earth shook and our brethren escaped, he was left behind. He wandered alone and lost for years without direction or hope" (655); "Tales of our young Warchief, Thrall, who bears the armor and hammer of Orgrim... A demonstration of our newly found shamanistic magic would give him new hope" (672); the raptor-eye amulet thrown to the ground: "What use have I for polished bone?... Mercy was denied me when I was not allowed to die with my Warchief in battle" (674, 675); the orb of raptor hearts (701, 702). The anchor (847), Gor'mul: "This... this power.... Is this the power of the shamans that Tor'gan tried to show me? I must say, it is interesting. Tell Tor'gan that he has convinced me... for the time being. I will listen to what he has to say"; Tor'gan: "When the enchantment wears off, I will speak with Gor'mul. My thanks to you for your help in returning Gor'mul to the land of the living."

**The voice.** A patient troll healer of spirits: warm, hopeful, honest about small steps. Speaks of the shamans' "new" power with pride.

**Never.** Gor'mul cured (the game keeps him as he was: one question is all), Doomhammer's death retold in detail, Zengu's sword (another chain, the same camp), a word for Gor'mul he wouldn't say.

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** Gor'mul left behind, years alone without hope, Doomhammer's fall, Thrall with Orgrim's armor and hammer, the shamans' new power, the amulet thrown down, the orb and the raptor's guile, convinced for the time being, the talk after the enchantment, back to the land of the living (655 to 847). **Ours.** The guile wearing off, the talk, the question about the young Warchief, the quiet, the little shown and not thrown down, the first question in years, the rest of the way theirs to walk.

## Letter

$N,

The raptor's guile wore off, as I knew it would. Then Gor'mul and I talked, the way I had hoped we might.

He listened. He did not smile, and I did not ask him to. He asked whether the young Warchief truly wears Orgrim's armor and carries his hammer, and I told him yes. He was quiet a long while after that. Then he asked what the shamans can do now, and I showed him a little, and he did not throw it on the ground.

That is not a warrior returned. But it is a warrior who asked a question, and in all the years he wandered alone, I do not think he asked anyone anything.

You brought him some of the way back, $N, and the rest of the way is his.

Tor'gan
Hammerfall
