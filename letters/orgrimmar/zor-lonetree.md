# Zor Lonetree

**Who.** Zor Lonetree (4047), Elder Far Seer, an orc in Orgrimmar, "well schooled in Alliance politics". The chain (1238 to 7541): the severed hand and the report of Marg Nighteye, who "spent weeks spying on the city of Theramore" (1238); his head, "Could a weapon have made that ragged cut, or did some beast slay our agent?" (1239); Kin'weelay at Grom'gol speaks with his spirit, "bound in our world, boiling with rage from a task yet undone" (1240); Marg: "I spied a group of Alliance soldiers camped on the beach. (1261); Nazeer: "this is a noble's pendant and we must discover its true owner" (1262); Zor: "This pendant was crafted with great skill and precision, $N. No orc would covet such a jewel... ## Letter

$N,

The scholars have the pendant, and they are slow, which is how scholars ought to be. I have looked at it myself many times. Humans put more of themselves into a jewel than an orc puts into a sword.

What I keep coming back to is the spy who took it. He watched Theramore for weeks, alone, and took the pendant on his final night, and a crab-man killed him on his way home. He did not finish his mission. You finished it for him. If spirits rest for such things, his should.

A far seer learns to wait, and I will wait on the pendant for as long as it asks. Our spy did not have that luxury. I am glad he had you.

Zor Lonetree
