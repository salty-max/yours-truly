# Neeru Fireblade

**Who.** Neeru Fireblade (3216), in the Cleft of Shadow in Orgrimmar: the leader of the Burning Blade, who believes he has Thrall's trust (5727). The chain (5726 to 5730, Hidden Enemies): Thrall sends the player to Skull Rock for a lieutenant's insignia of the Burning Blade, then to Neeru wearing it, to be taken for one of his own; then into Ragefire Chasm to kill Bazzalan and Jergosh the Invoker, the Searing Blade's leaders, "be careful not to let Neeru know it was you" (5728). Back with Neeru (5729): "What is it?! Oh, you, $C... my apologies. My anger rivals that of a rabid kodo bull... but perhaps it is my own fault. In sending travelers into Ragefire Chasm, I should have seen the possibility that some harm would come from it. It seems both Bazzalan and Jergosh were taken unaware and slain by some of Thrall's do-gooders." The anchor (5730): "You are one of my lieutenants! Prepare yourself, $c. I will call upon you soon... I will do what I can to minimize the damage here in Ragefire Chasm. In the meantime, I will have my agents in the Barrens and Ashenvale begin work on our newest agenda." The player tells Thrall everything. Thrall never writes (the plan's rule): the letter is the enemy's, to the spy he takes for his own.

**The voice.** A conspirator's letter, veiled in case other eyes read it: no name of the Blade, the Council or the Chasm ("below the city", "our friends", "the forest"), courteous, warm to a lieutenant, menace kept under the manners. Calls the player by their class, as he does. The irony is the reader's: he writes fondly to Thrall's agent.

**Never.** A summons or a task (his "I will call upon you soon" stays in the game), the Burning Blade, the Shadow Council or Ragefire Chasm named, a hint he suspects the player, Thrall's next moves, the Searing Blade said gone for good.

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** The temper of a rabid kodo bull, his own fault, sending travelers below, Bazzalan and Jergosh slain by Thrall's do-gooders, minimizing the damage below, his agents in the Barrens and Ashenvale (the forest), the lieutenant, the insignia (5726 to 5730). **Ours.** The apology on paper, wanting their names, the tidying, the right mark at the right moment, the comfort of one who is what they seem, signing as a friend in the Cleft.

## Letter

$C,

Forgive my temper when you came to see me. It rivaled a rabid kodo bull's, and you had done nothing to earn it. The fault was mine. I sent travelers below the city without a thought for who else might follow them, and two friends paid for my carelessness. Thrall's do-gooders, whoever they were. I would give a great deal for their names.

I am tidying what I can below. Our friends in the Barrens and in the forest have begun their own work.

You came to me at the right moment, wearing the right mark. I do not forget that. In a city where so few are what they seem, it is a comfort to know one who is.

A friend in the Cleft
