## Letter

$N,

I sent Belathaan to the Shrine of Akir because logic told me to. Talk costs less than blood, and peace less than war. Every line of that reasoning was sound. The cult answered it with a box. I have gone over my arguments many times since, looking for the error, and I can only find it in my estimate of the people I sent him to.

The Windshapers were right. Writing that down is worse than saying it.

The charms you brought me are locked away. I look at them less than I did. I have not forgotten who brought them.

The cult weighed our prudence and called it weakness. That was an estimate too, and theirs is the worse one.

Elaadrin Evengale
Supreme Magister of the High Order
