# Falorne Fallwind

**Who.** Falorne Fallwind (251371), of the High Order (the Skyborne who play the Alliance), in Thendal Village on Zephras Isle, where every Skyborne begins. Before the anchor, Rorian the Dayseeker (251361) gives the new shen'dorei the relic of their coming of age, one of many passed down, "Someday when your ashes are scattered to the winds it will pass to another... know that the winds of home will be with you wherever you travel" (92460), and sends them to thin the vuldren, which the magic on the winds has made restless (92461). The anchor, Reading the Ley Lines (92597, the High Order's racial lesson, level 4): the ley lines under Zephras "have begun to pierce the surface", and Falorne sends the player to the standing stones to draw on one with their birthright. "Do you feel it? Yes? Good! You have taken your first steps... making the unknown known. If we are to survive these dire times, we must be unwavering in our search for knowledge... and power." The first letter a High Order Skyborne gets. Forever only: the texts are Wowhead's of the beta (wow-data, 23 October 2026), to read again at launch.

**The voice.** A teacher of the arcane who answers his own questions before the student can ("Do you feel it? Yes? Good!"), quick, sure, delighted by what can be measured. The High Order's hunger shows once, in the order he puts things: knowledge, then power. Kind to Rorian, unconvinced by him.

**Never.** A hedge (Denalan hedges, Falorne decides), the Windshapers (no quest of Falorne's names them), the island's later troubles (the cult, Valanaar), a change the player could look for at the stones. A man (his model: DisplaySexID 0, as Rorian's, whom the quests call "he").

**French.** Vouvoie. Rorian's words are the French client's ("les vents de votre patrie vous accompagneront partout où vous irez"), and "rendre l'inconnu connu" is Falorne's own.

**The game's.** The relic and Rorian's words (92460), the ley lines piercing the surface, the standing stones, the birthright, "making the unknown known", knowledge and power, dire times (92597), the shen'dorei (92460). **Ours.** The hum that stays, the old books, gift or warning as knowledge either way, the preference for what can be measured, knowledge first and power after.

## Letter

$N,

Do you still feel it, the hum near the standing stones? Yes? Good. Then it took, and you will feel it again in many places.

Ley lines used to live in old books. Now they come up through the grass to meet us. Gift or warning, it is knowledge, and I mean to have all of it before anyone else decides what I may know.

Rorian gave you your relic and told you the winds of home would be with you wherever you travel. Rorian believes that, and it is a kind thing to believe. I prefer what I can measure.

These are hard times for the shen'dorei, and I know which I want first: knowledge, then power.

Make the unknown known,
Falorne Fallwind
