# Ventaari Brightwish

**Who.** Ventaari Brightwish (251487), of the Windshapers (the Skyborne who play the Horde), in Thendal Village on Zephras Isle, where every Skyborne begins. The anchor, The Gift of Skysight (92598, the Windshapers' racial lesson, level 4, a quest alone): the skysight is "sacred... gifted to us by the elemental spirits"; Ventaari sends the player to the elemental convergence at the standing stones northeast of the village, where "you may at times receive other bits of wisdom from the spirits that are drawn to such places". Then: "Like a muscle, your skysight must be exercised if you hope to grow stronger in the gift... You'd do well to remember what you've learned here today. Our ability to use our gifts to hear what the elements tell us could be the key to our survival." The first letter a Windshaper Skyborne gets. A woman (her model: DisplaySexID 1, as Ayessa's, whom the French quests make "déçue"). Forever only: the texts are Wowhead's of the beta (wow-data, 23 October 2026), to read again at launch.

**The voice.** A coach of a sense: plain, firm, practical, warm under it. Thinks in practice and repetition, the muscle her own image. Reverent about the spirits without ceremony: listening is work, done every day. Short imperatives, then the reason.

**Never.** Ceremony or blessings (Mull's and Hawkwind's ground), the High Order (the quest doesn't name them), the island's later troubles, Belathaan Brightwish (the same family name, but no source says they're kin, and his death is a later chain's), a place she is said to stand where the game doesn't keep her (she stays in the village).

**French.** Vouvoie, never a participle that agrees with the reader ("Avez-vous utilisé", not "Vous en êtes-vous servi"). The muscle is the French client's own image ("La vue céleste est comme un muscle").

**The game's.** The skysight, a sacred gift of the spirits, the convergence at the standing stones, the spirits' bits of wisdom, the muscle, remembering what was learned, listening to the elements as the key to survival (92598). **Ours.** Using it everywhere, the quiet days, her evenings in the village before she sleeps, the voice through a wall, the notes she keeps, needing everyone who can listen.

## Letter

$N,

A gift from the spirits is still a muscle, and a muscle left alone goes soft. Use your skysight on the road, at the inn, in the rain. Most days you will see nothing worth seeing. Use it anyway.

I keep mine open a little every evening, here in the village, before I sleep. Some evenings I catch a word or two, faint as a voice through a wall. Some evenings, nothing. I have learned not to resent the quiet ones. They are part of the practice too.

I write down what I catch, even when it makes no sense. Especially then. The spirits do not repeat themselves for our convenience.

We will need everyone who can listen.

Patience,
Ventaari Brightwish
