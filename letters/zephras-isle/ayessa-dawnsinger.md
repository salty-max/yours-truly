# Ayessa Dawnsinger

**Who.** Ayessa Dawnsinger (251968), Grand Skyseer of the Windshapers, in their lodge in Valanaar. The chain before the anchor, as the Windshapers walk it: the western watchtower's peacekeepers dead and the pylon violated (93926, 93927), Shen'dar Village and the Al'Aketh cult (92514 to 92550), Valanaar and the Grand Skyseer: "Fewer and fewer of us have the skysight of late... I look forward to further making your acquaintance" (92700); the Broken Construct, one of the few gifts of the wind spirits still working, its parts taken, and the name the construct gives, Belathaan Brightwish of the High Order: "I know Belathaan. He's Valennia Stormfist's cousin and his mother was actually a Windshaper. It's disappointing that he was involved in this. I need to reflect on this for a moment" (93735 to 93738). A Firm Response (93746): she sends the player to stop Belathaan on the road to the Shrine of Akir and "put a bit of fear into his heart". He is dead. "The High Order was meeting with the Al'Aketh to discuss peace? There is no peace with the cult... Pay this no mind, $N. You are not at fault for this... You were merely in the right place at the wrong time." The anchor, Blood for Blood (93740, level 11): Windshaper novices found dead in the Highlands, "a strong chance that the Al'Aketh are responsible. Windshapers trade blood for blood, every time... We do not forgive and we do not forget." And when the player brings the cult's charms: "<Ayessa looks shaken and distant>... Don't mind me, $N. Today has merely been... a trying day." A big letter, the island's story for its side (up to 180 words). Forever only: the texts are Wowhead's of the beta (wow-data, 23 October 2026), to read again at launch.

**The voice.** Fierce, clipped, an oath-taker: her people's creed in her mouth, short and hard. Then the crack the anchor showed: she weighs blame like a judge who has found herself on the list. Honest past comfort. The letter is the answer she didn't give that day.

**Never.** Certainty she doesn't have (the novices' killers were "a strong chance"), Lorthuna or anything of the Shrine's climax (later quests, and the cutscene's kinship is wiki-only), forgiveness offered, the player blamed, a word that agrees with the reader in French.

**French.** Vouvoie. The creed is the French client's ("Les Sculpte-vents répondent au sang par le sang", "Ni oubli ni pardon"), and the last line turns it on Belathaan in both languages ("I will not forget him either"). Ayessa is a woman in the game's French ("Je suis déçue"), though the letter needs no word that agrees with her.

**The game's.** Blood for blood, every time, we do not forgive and we do not forget, the novices in the Highlands, the charms, the trying day (93740), Belathaan sent after on the road, his death, not the player's fault, the High Order's peace talks (93746), his mother a Windshaper (93738). **Ours.** The better answer owed, still meaning it, the blame weighed three ways and herself on the list, not forgetting him either.

## Letter

$N,

I owe you a better answer than the one I gave you when you brought me the charms. "A trying day" was not it.

Windshapers trade blood for blood. I have said it all my life, and I meant it when I sent you north. I still mean it. The novices we found in the Highlands were ours, and someone will answer for every one of them.

But I had also sent you after Belathaan, to frighten him, and he never came back from that road. I told you it was not your fault. That was true. I did not say whose it was, because I have not decided. Elaadrin's, for sending him. The cult's, for killing him. Mine, for wanting him afraid. His mother was one of us.

We do not forgive and we do not forget. I will not forget him either.

Ayessa Dawnsinger
Grand Skyseer of the Windshapers
