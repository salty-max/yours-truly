# Advisor Belgrum

**Who.** Advisor Belgrum (2918), a dwarf, an advisor to the king in Ironforge. The chain (720 to 1139): Hammertoe Grez's chalk message, "They're taking me to Uldaman. Send help" (720); Prospector Ryedol, Hammertoe found dying in Uldaman, his amulet taken back from Magregan Deepshadow, "He knows I was seeking the origins of our people, and... it was also my mission to stop the Shadowforge clan" (721 to 723); Ryedol: "I think my spirits were higher when I thought he was dead" (723); Historian Karnik: "We will have to take time to mourn his death later" (724), "I will take the time to speak to Hammertoe's spirit" (725); Belgrum: "Och, these are evil tidings... on the king's authority" (726); Ambassador Infernus slain at Angor Fortress, sent there by Ragnaros (762). The anchor (1139): "Hammertoe's quest is still unfulfilled. His death must not be in vain. He dreamt of finding the Tablet of Will: a stone shard inscribed with magic by the titans themselves to help imbue their creations with life"; and: "<Advisor Belgrum shakes his head in disbelief as you hand him the stone shard.> $N, this is amazing. I shall have this brought to the king's mages as soon as possible. They will want to study it to find out if the legends are true. But that is a discussion for another time: you have aided our people more than I would have ever expected--thank you."

**The voice.** A dwarf of the court: formal on paper, "Och" when it slips, careful about what is the mages' to say. Moved by Hammertoe more than an advisor should admit.

**Never.** What the mages learn (later, unknown), the king quoted, Hammertoe's spirit's words as fact (Karnik's to tell), the Shadowforge gone, the titans' secrets explained.

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** Hammertoe, the origins of their people, stopping the Shadowforge, the amulet, Karnik who speaks to his spirit, evil tidings, Infernus, the Tablet of Will and its legend, the king's mages, his death not in vain (720 to 1139). **Ours.** The tablet under more locks than he has keys, Karnik's word that Hammertoe is content and choosing to believe it, a life worth an advisor's letter.

## Letter

$N,

The tablet is with the king's mages, under more locks than I have keys for. What they learn from it is theirs to say. What I can say is that it never reached Shadowforge hands, and that it was Hammertoe's dream long before it was anyone's business.

Karnik tells me he has spoken with Hammertoe's spirit. I am an advisor, not a priest, and I do not know what to make of that. But he says Hammertoe is content, and I have decided to believe him.

Och. A dwarf who spent his life looking for where our people came from, and the last of it making sure the wrong hands never found it first. I'd not have done half so well, in his place or in yours.

That is a life worth an advisor's letter, and you are the reason it was not wasted.

Advisor Belgrum
Ironforge
