# Marin Noggenfogger

**Who.** Marin Noggenfogger (7564), a goblin in Gadgetzan, Tanaris, and the man of the elixir. The chain (2605 to 2662): "I've heard of some strange creatures called dew collectors... able to store water in special glands. I think that if I could get one of these glands, I could create a drink that would quench my terrible thirst!" (2605); "Blech! This stuff tastes terrible! So much for that idea... I mean, if it's going to taste that bad, it at least should have some kick to it" (2606); Sprinkle: "Marin's a good guy, don't get me wrong -- just a little... eccentric, I'd say" (2606); the Violet Tragan from the Hinterlands, Sprinkle's secret ingredient, "Take it to Marin and tell him he owes me a favor!" (2641, 2661). The anchor (2662): "Drink this! It's absolutely wonderful!!! I knew I had it in me... please, stand a little to the side, will you? You're blocking my business! I'm going to make millions on the fabulous Noggenfogger Elixir, millions! What's that? Sprinkle says I owe her a favor? Oh, that Sprinkle, such a kidder!" The elixir's effects (smaller, bonier, falling slowly) are its own spells in the game.

**The voice.** A delighted goblin salesman, a little dim, wholly sincere: numbers rounded up, side effects rounded down. Unlike Crank, nothing sly: he believes every word.

**Never.** Sprinkle laughing at him as the joke (her line is the game's: the letter keeps it his), the millions made (hundreds, climbing), a debt paid or owed to the player, a statue the player could look for.

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** The dew gland and the terrible thirst, it tasted terrible, some kick to it, Sprinkle and her secret ingredient, the Violet Tragan, the fabulous Noggenfogger Elixir, millions, blocking his business, the favour and such a kidder (2605 to 2662). **Ours.** Hundreds and climbing, customers back smaller or bonier, the genius in the stirring, the dew collector's taste at the beginning, remembering it when he is famous.

## Letter

$N,

Millions! Well. Thousands, nearly. Well, a very encouraging number of hundreds, and climbing!

The fabulous Noggenfogger Elixir is selling better than anything in Gadgetzan that isn't water. Customers come back for more, which is the sign of a quality product. Some of them come back a little smaller, or a little bonier, or floating down from somewhere, but they come back, and that's what counts.

Sprinkle keeps telling people I owe her a favour. Funny woman. The powder was her idea, sure, but the genius was in the stirring.

You were there at the very beginning, $N, when it tasted like the inside of a dew gland, and I'll remember it when I'm famous.

Marin Noggenfogger
Gadgetzan
