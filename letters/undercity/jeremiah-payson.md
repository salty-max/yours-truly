# Jeremiah Payson

**Who.** Jeremiah Payson (8403), the cockroach vendor of the Undercity, a Forsaken, born Jeremiah Felstone. The chain (5021, 5023, 5049, 5050, 5051): Janice Felstone's ghost on the Felstone Farm and her package, "addressed in a hurried hand to a Jeremiah Felstone of Lordaeron; the delivery of the package is dated for well over four years ago" (5023); the census: "some of the Forsaken have changed their last names to rid themselves of their former countenance... the former Jeremiah Felstone is now known as Jeremiah Payson. Wait a minute... isn't he the cockroach vendor?" (5023). Jeremiah: "I haven't used that name in years... but yes, at one point in my life - before this glorious unlife - that was my last name. You claim to have known my sister... or rather, have recently spoken to my sister. Who's been dead. For four years. You know, if my bones weren't already brittle enough, I'd be tempted to break my foot off on your posterior. You're quite the vulture, aren't you?" (5049); "It would seem that I was wrong about you $N... My sister sent me a number of personal effects, including humorously enough the deed to the Felstone farm... half of a good luck charm she had, with her husband having the other half. Maybe her knowing I have these things will now put her soul to rest" (5050); the charm made whole and Janice freed (5051), which he never sees. The Horde's road (5049) and the end (5051): Ol' Emma writes the Alliance's.

**The voice.** A sardonic Forsaken shopkeeper: dry, prickly, proud of his new name and his trade, hiding a soft spot under the cockroach crates. Where Emma takes her name back, he keeps his new one. Terse by card: under 100 words, and the letter reads complete.

**Never.** Janice's last words or her husband named (he never heard them), the ghost's rest as fact, Emma (another side, another letter), the plague told, Samsa's glee (another Forsaken, another voice).

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** Felstone left behind for Payson, this glorious unlife, the sister dead four years, the vulture, being wrong about the player, the personal effects and the deed, the half-charm and her husband's half, her soul at rest maybe (5021 to 5051). **Ours.** Still Payson and saying so first, minding being wrong less than expected, all her letters kept in a box under the cockroach crates, where no one will look.

## Letter

$N,

I am still Payson. I want that understood before anything else. I took the name after I woke into this glorious unlife, and a parcel from the dead does not change my mind.

I called you a vulture. You were not one, as it turned out, and I find I mind being wrong less than I expected.

But I kept what she sent. All of it. It is all in a box under the cockroach crates, where no one will think to look, which is rather the point.

Jeremiah Payson
Cockroach Vendor
