# Sharlindra

**Who.** Sharlindra (2227), a banshee of the Dark Lady's court in the Royal Quarter of the Undercity, a woman. The chain (518 to 521): Melisara in Tarren Mill against Mug'Thol and the Crushridge ogres who overran the ruins of Alterac; his lieutenants' heads, "When you bring me Mug'Thol's head, I will have the four of them mounted on pikes above Alterac. A fitting monument to those who go against the Dark Lady's wishes" (519); Mug'Thol and the Crown of Will (520). The anchor (521), Melisara: "Deliver the Crown of Will to Sharlindra in the Undercity. She will see that it is delivered to Lady Sylvanas. I would warn you against thinking to use it for your own purposes, $n, Mug'Thol's fate should be illustration enough"; Sharlindra: "So this is the relic the ogre used to duck the will of the Dark Lady... Strange that something so simple could wield so great a power. And to fall into as clumsy hands as those. Well done, $N, I will deliver the crown to Lady Sylvanas, and see that she hears of your accomplishment."

**The voice.** A courtier of the dead: elegant, cold, amused, sharp about power and its appearances. Intimate with the throne and careful with it. A banshee's dry wit about the living.

**Never.** The Dark Lady quoted or her thoughts told (a leader never writes, and isn't put in a letter's mouth), the crown's use or fate after, the pikes (Melisara's, and the game shows none), Mug'Thol gone for good.

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** The Crown of Will, the ogre ducking the Dark Lady's will, something so simple with so great a power, clumsy hands, delivered to Lady Sylvanas, seeing that she hears of the player (518 to 521). **Ours.** Carrying it herself, held away like something that might bite, power that ought to look the part, the player's name spoken in the Royal Quarter, more than most of the living can say.

## Letter

$N,

A clumsy ogre kept himself out of the Dark Lady's reach for as long as he did, with nothing but a crown. I find that more unsettling than if it had been made of gold. Great power ought at least to have the decency to look the part.

The crown has been delivered. I carried it to the Dark Lady myself, and I confess I held it a little away from me, as one carries something that might bite.

She has heard of your part in it. I saw to that, as I said I would. Your name has been spoken in the Royal Quarter, which is more than most of the living can say.

Sharlindra
