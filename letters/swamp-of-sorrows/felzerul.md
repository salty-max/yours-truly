# Fel'zerul

**Who.** Fel'zerul (1443), an orc in Stonard, in the Swamp of Sorrows. The chain (1424 to 1445): "The Warchief sent us to investigate strange happenings at The Temple of Atal'Hakkar. The Atal'ai trolls are a tribe of dark casters, known for practicing forbidden magic. You can imagine how astonished we were when we arrived here to discover the temple had been sunk. The green dragons now guard the site, claiming to be protectors of Azeroth" (1424); a scroll "written in blood on dried flesh... a judicial sentence against a priest of the Atal'ai tribe... exiled to live far north in the Hinterlands, with the Witherbark trolls" (1429); the exile: "Jammal'an the Prophet sentenced me to exile... When I expressed my concerns, I was labeled an infidel and ousted from the temple" (1444). The anchor (1445): "The Atal'ai spiritual leader believes once Hakkar returns to Azeroth from the Nether, the god will grant the Atal'ai tribe immortality. Foolish trickery to bring about a premature doomsday if you ask me... Venture into the Temple, and seize the fetishes!"; and: "Brave $C, you have proven yourself to be a great champion of the Horde. Now this collection of fetishes of Hakkar must be dealt with at once!"

**The voice.** A sharp, skeptical orc investigator: dry, impatient with prophecy, a soldier's respect for the man who spoke up. Calls the player by their class, as he does.

**Never.** Hakkar's return or the temple's fate (later, a dungeon's), Jammal'an defeated (he stays in his temple), the green dragons' purpose explained, the fetishes anywhere the player could look.

**French.** Vouvoie. Stonard is "Pierrêche" on Forever's French client, Classic Era's name swapped in.

**The game's.** The Warchief's errand, the sunken temple, the green dragons guarding it, the Atal'ai and forbidden magic, the sentence written in blood on dried flesh, the exile among the Witherbark labeled an infidel for his concerns, Jammal'an's prophecy of immortality, a premature doomsday, the fetishes dealt with at once (1424 to 1445). **Ours.** The fetishes burned upwind at the Pool of Tears, the long smoke, the man every army has who says what he sees, the green dragons watching the water and so is he.

## Letter

$C,

The fetishes are ash. I burned them myself at the edge of the Pool of Tears, and stood upwind. Whatever was in them took a long time to stop smoking.

I keep thinking about that exile in the Hinterlands. He looked at his high priest's prophecy and said what he saw, and they wrote his sentence in blood on dried flesh and sent him to live among the Witherbark. Every army I have served in had a man like that. Most of them were right.

Jammal'an still believes his god will make the Atal'ai immortal, and the green dragons are still watching the water.

Fel'zerul
Stonard
