# Carlin Redpath

**Who.** Carlin Redpath (11063), of the Argent Dawn, at Light's Hope Chapel in the Eastern Plaguelands, a man, who survived the fall of Darrowshire. The chain (5149 to 5181): Pamela, a child's ghost in Darrowshire waiting for her father, and her doll (5149); her Auntie Marlene, "She doesn't know what happened to her father, does she? My brother, Joseph?" (5152); "His soul was twisted by the Scourge, and he became a monster" (5153); Chromie and the Annals of Darrowshire from Andorhal's city hall: "during the battle, Joseph Redpath was corrupted and joined the ranks of the Scourge! He then betrayed the defenders of Darrowshire" (5154); the Annals grown new pages, "another Redpath, Carlin, who survived Joseph's betrayal" (5210); Carlin: "That was my home before the war... my home before the battle that destroyed it!... Here is a passage about me! And one about my brother Joseph! Is this book true? We must discover the truth!" (5210). The anchor (5181): the skull of Horgus the Ravager from the lake by Corin's Crossing, the shattered sword of Marduk the Black from the gorge; "These relics hold a power I do not fully understand, a power you will one day harness. They also bring dark memories to my mind, memories death and treachery, and of the last night of Darrowshire..."

**The voice.** A survivor and a soldier of the Dawn: quiet, honest, haunted, plain words carrying a weight. The book told him what he didn't know about his own brother.

**Never.** Pamela's ghost (he doesn't know of her: the player met her, he didn't), the battle's outcome changed (later quests, Chromie's), the relics' power used (a promise for later), Marduk or Horgus said beaten.

**French.** Vouvoie. Darrowshire is "Comté-de-Darrow" on Forever's French client, Classic Era's name swapped in.

**The game's.** Darrowshire his home before the battle that destroyed it, the Annals and their new pages, Joseph corrupted and the betrayal, the passage about himself, the skull of Horgus and the sword of Marduk, a power not understood, the dark memories, death, treachery and the last night (5149 to 5181). **Ours.** Not sleeping well, his home written by someone who wasn't there and then by something stranger, not knowing whose hand let them in, the relics locked away and the feeling when he touches them, Joseph's daughter not thought of in years.

## Letter

$N,

I have not slept well since you brought me that book. The Annals of Darrowshire. My home, written down by someone who was not there, and then written further by something I do not understand.

It says my brother Joseph turned. That he was corrupted, and betrayed the defenders, and that they died for it. I was one of the defenders. I lived. Until I read it, I did not know who had betrayed us.

The skull and the broken sword are locked away. I do not like what I feel when I touch them, so I do not touch them.

Joseph had a daughter. I have not let myself think of her in years.

Carlin Redpath
Light's Hope Chapel
