# Nathanos Blightcaller

**Who.** Nathanos Blightcaller (11878), at the Marris Stead, the farm where he died. The anchor is Ramstein (6163), which requires Duskwing (6135, the albino demon bat, whose fur he promised to "fashion into something you can use") and Borelgore (6136, the maggot beast, after which he promotes the player "from imbecile to mental deficient, first class"). In Ramstein he tells how the Scourge took the stead, how he woke as Scourge, how the Dark Lady came for him, how he hunted the beast for months before it fled into Stratholme. Then he says he'll hang its head on his wall, and makes the player one of his champions. The chain began with feeding his hounds on living rot (6022).

**The voice.** Precise, cold, short. He resents having to acknowledge anyone and does it nonetheless. His insults are ranks, and the player has climbed them. A few plain sentences make the contempt sharper than an insult in every line. He is attentive to his hounds. Writes "$C," as he speaks. The shortest of the six samples.

**Never.** Sentiment without a sting, his life before beyond what the quest says (nothing from later lore), a word against the Dark Lady, a guess at Duskwing's reward (gloves or a mantle).

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** The head and the champion's place (6163), the months spent hunting Ramstein and its retreat to Stratholme (6163's request), the farm's condition, the hounds and living rot (6022), "imbecile" (his word for the player from 6022 to 6136). **Ours.** The moved bowls, the rank put in writing, what passes for a head. No reward is assumed to be worn, no revenge is said to restore the farm and nothing is enclosed.

## Letter

$C,

Ramstein's head is on the wall. The hounds will eat living rot without complaint. They will not eat beneath that head. I have moved their bowls.

I hunted Ramstein for months before it fled into Stratholme. Having it here has done little for the state of the farm. I did not expect it to. I wanted the thing dead, and you saw to it.

You came to my stead an imbecile. You are now one of my champions. I put that in writing because you would not believe it otherwise. Try not to let it swell what passes for your head.

There. You have had it.

Nathanos
