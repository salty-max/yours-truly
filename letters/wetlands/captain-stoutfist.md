# Captain Stoutfist

**Who.** Captain Stoutfist (2104), a dwarf, who commands Menethil Harbor from the war room of its keep in the Wetlands. The chain (455 to 474): the Dragonmaw push into Dun Algaz, "Loch Modan will be cut off from Menethil Harbor, our port and stronghold in the Wetlands", the report to Valstag Ironjaw (455); "You're a $R of worth" (473); their war banners, and their history: "all but destroyed by the end of the Second War, after they lost control of their red dragon pets", led by Nek'rosh, son of Nekros, sworn to revenge on the dragons (464); the catapults built to lay siege to Menethil, burned with tinder, "cut this Dragonmaw beast down...at its neck" (465). The anchor (474): "Stoutly done, $N. You have dealt a telling blow to the Dragonmaw orc clan. They may never recover. And the destruction of those catapults has made Menethil Harbor safe. You are a $R of merit, and the kingdom of Ironforge rewards those who serve it well."

**The voice.** A dwarven officer: gruff, short, practical, proud of his harbor. Thinks in siege terms and supply lines. Praise given rarely and meant. Calls the player by their race, as he does.

**Never.** Nek'rosh or the Dragonmaw gone for good (both come back), the harbor's ships or guards changed in a way the player could look for, the dragons of Grim Batol (a later story), Regthar's soldier's pride (his own is the harbor's).

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** The harbor cut off and then safe, Valstag, the war banners, Nek'rosh and Nekros, the catapults and the tinder, the telling blow, "they may never recover", a $R of merit (455 to 474). **Ours.** Ships tying up without watching the hills, a siege engine as a patient thing, watching the smoke from the keep wall, still watching the hills, saying it in front of anyone.

## Letter

$N,

The harbor's open. Ships in, ships out, and not one crew watching the hills while it ties up. That's your doing.

I'll tell you what I told Valstag. A siege engine is a patient thing. It sits in the hills and waits for you to get comfortable. Nek'rosh had his waiting for us, and you put a bit of tinder to every one of them. I watched the smoke from the keep wall and didn't say a word for a good while.

The Dragonmaw aren't done. They'll lick their wounds in those hills a long time, and I'm watching. But they'll do it looking at a harbor that's still standing.

A $R of merit, I called you. I'd call you that again in front of anyone.

Captain Stoutfist
Menethil Harbor
