# Thyn'tel Bladeweaver

**Who.** Thyn'tel Bladeweaver (8026), one of the commanders of the Sentinels, in Darnassus, a woman. The chain (1022 to 1044): the Tome of Mel'Thandris at the Howling Vale ("not a book in the traditional sense... records events of import in its pages") shows the player that Priestess Velinde Starsong received the Scythe of Elune; Thyn'tel, guarded ("it would take good reason for me to divulge such information"), opens Velinde's chest in the Sentinels' bunkhouse: "the Sentinels believe that she had her own reasons for leaving, and expect that she could return at any time... I cannot believe that she has come to a bad end" (1038). The trail runs through Ratchet, Booty Bay and Darkshire to the Carevin family and the worgen in a Duskwood mine (1039 to 1043). The anchor, Answered Questions (1044): "So this is the fate of Velinde, is it? The high priestess will be disappointed to hear of this, they were good friends before her disappearance... the Scythe of Elune... it is unsettling to me that it has fallen out of her servants' hands. Another matter for another time, however. I owe you my thanks in no small amount."

**The voice.** Discreet and loyal: a commander who weighs what may be said, keeps faith with her people, and writes less than she feels. The restraint is the voice. The one admission she allows herself is a wrong belief.

**Never.** The high priestess named or quoted (a leader never writes, and isn't put in a letter's mouth either), what the Scythe will lead to (later quests), Velinde judged, a hint at a next errand.

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** The high priestess and Velinde good friends, Velinde's chest in the bunkhouse, the Sentinels' belief she would return, the Scythe out of her servants' hands unsettling her, the thanks in no small amount, the Tome that records events (1022 to 1044). **Ours.** Telling the high priestess and keeping what passed, the long way back, the chest she can't empty, wrong about this above all, the world crossed on a book's word.

## Letter

$N,

I have told the high priestess. What passed between us I will keep. I will say only that I took the long way back to the bunkhouse.

Velinde's chest is still in the bunkhouse. I find I cannot have it emptied. The Sentinels believed she had her own reasons for leaving and would come back when she chose. I believed it too, and said so to you. I would rather have been wrong about almost anything else.

It unsettles me that the Scythe of Elune has fallen out of her servants' hands.

You followed her across half the world on the word of a book that writes itself. Few would have. I owe you my thanks, in no small amount.

Thyn'tel Bladeweaver
