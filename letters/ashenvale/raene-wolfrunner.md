# Raene Wolfrunner

**Who.** Raene Wolfrunner (3691), a Sentinel of Astranaar in Ashenvale, a woman. The chain (991 to 1025): her "longtime friend" Teronis, who sought a gem for a rod that might slow the furbolg attacks, has not come back; the player finds his body broken on the island in Lake Falathim, his journal, and the gem (991, 1023): "This is terrible, $N. Teronis is dead?! The time for mourning will come, but for now we must focus our efforts... I would rest much better knowing his death was not in vain." The anchor, An Aggressive Defense (1025): the furbolgs attacking travelers on the road east before the Falfarren River, "show those monsters the same mercy they have shown our kind", and, waiting: "The furbolg were not always our enemy, $N. But times change, and it is no longer a time of peace here in these dark woods." The moonwell, Teronis's task finished, is a later quest the anchor doesn't require.

**The voice.** A Sentinel: duty first, grief held behind it, few words and each one weighed. Honest about the orders she gives. Writes once the mourning has caught up with her.

**Never.** Teronis's task called finished (the moonwell is later), his body brought home (the game keeps it on the island), the road said safe, an old friendship with the furbolg invented (only her own memory of a quieter time).

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** Teronis, her friend, his death, the journal and the gem the player found, mourning that will come, the furbolg not always enemies, the road east, defense (991 to 1025), Elune (her people's). **Ours.** The mourning come at no chosen hour, the shortest letter to his family, more than she dared hope and not enough, never drawing a bow on one when she was young, wanting it written.

## Letter

$N,

The time for mourning has come, as I said it would, and it keeps no hour I would choose. I wrote to Teronis's family. It was the shortest letter I have ever written, and it took me all night.

You found him, and you found what he died looking for. It was more than I dared hope, and it is not enough.

The furbolg were not always our enemy. When I was young, I never once drew a bow on one. Now I have sent you down the road to their camps and called it defense, and it was. I only want it written somewhere that I remember the quieter time.

Elune keep you,
Raene Wolfrunner
