# Rejold Barleybrew

**Who.** Rejold Barleybrew (1374), a brewer in Brewnall Village: "I'm on a quest, a quest to make the perfect stout. I know I can do it -- brewing's in my blood." The player brought him Shimmerweed from the Frostmane trolls (315), then carried his first Barrel of Shimmer Stout to his brother, Mountaineer Barleybrew (1959), at the southern guard tower on the border to Loch Modan, "not a brewer like the rest of us Barleybrews" (413: "This stuff really puts a shine in your eyes. I feel like I can see in the dark!"). The brother sent a cask on with the player to Mountaineer Kadrell (1340) in Thelsamar (414, the anchor: "Magni's Beard! This stuff gets your head turning! And it lightens the limbs, too. I feel like I can fly!"). The brother's name, Wellart, is only in the English quest text, so the letter says "my brother". Rejold's New Brew (415) is another branch.

**The voice.** A craftsman talking shop: tastes, malt, batches, the family trade. Proud of the stout, quoting its reviews like a man who has heard them read out a dozen times, and still not satisfied, because "perfect" is the word he chose. Cheerful and earnest, not loud (Nesingwary is the one who booms). Calls the player "$glad:lass;" once. Ends where a brewer would, on the next batch.

**Never.** A drink saved for the player (nothing to wait for), more shimmerweed wanted (no errand), the stout declared perfect, the brother's English-only name, the other brew (415).

**French.** Vouvoie, with a rustic "$gmon gars:ma fille;" once, as a country dwarf would.

**The game's.** The perfect stout and brewing in his blood, the shimmerweed (315), the brother, the barrel, the border tower, "puts a shine in your eyes... see in the dark" (413), Kadrell in Thelsamar, "lightens the limbs... I feel like I can fly!" (414). **Ours.** Kadrell's word sent back, living on the reviews, the stout not perfect yet, his theory of the malt, three small batches.

## Letter

Hail, $N!

My brother says the Shimmer Stout puts a shine in your eyes and he can see in the dark. Mountaineer Kadrell sent word from Thelsamar that it lightens the limbs and he feels like he can fly. A brewer could live on words like that, and I nearly have. Thank you for carrying it to them, $glad:lass;.

Here's the trouble. It isn't perfect. A stout that makes a dwarf feel he can fly is a fine stout. A perfect stout keeps his boots on the ground and makes him glad of it. The shimmerweed is the right note. It's the malt underneath that's wrong, too sweet or not dark enough, and I've three small batches going to find out.

Brewing's in my blood, and blood is patient. I'll get there.

Rejold Barleybrew
