# Lotwil Veriatus

**Who.** Lotwil Veriatus (2921), a gnome mage and inventor camped in the Dustbowl in the Badlands. The chain (710 to 716): "Here in the Badlands I've stopped to work on my latest project: my shackles of elemental binding. When complete, they will allow mages to summon even more powerful elementals for use in even greater capacities" (710); the small stone shards, "Hmm, a bit more sand than I'd anticipated. Have to adjust the coefficient of friction. No, wait; it should still be negligible with the frost oil" (710); the large stone slabs, "I would have thought it'd take half a dozen tauren to bring all these to me" (711); five bracers of rock binding "just so I have extras. Servo's notorious for misplacing my reagents and research"; "have you ever considered becoming a research assistant?" (712). The anchor (716), his assistant Lucien Tosselwrench: "I know how cheap Lotwil can be, $N, so let me make you something for all your troubles. Maybe it'll even help protect you from the coming atrocity that is Lotwil's newest invention... I've been working with all the extra stones you collected for him."

**The voice.** An absent, brilliant, oblivious gnome: thinks aloud mid-sentence, corrects himself, means well, never thinks of paying. Unlike Remtravel, he is not lost: he is elsewhere, inside his work.

**Never.** The shackles' first test told (later quests), a research post offered (an invitation the game doesn't keep), Servo mocked, Gaxim's Assembly.

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** The shackles of elemental binding, mages summoning more powerful elementals, the shards and slabs and bracers, the sand and the coefficient, five bracers because of Servo, the research assistant, Lucien and the extra stones, Lotwil's cheapness (710 to 716). **Ours.** A brief note in the middle of something, two bracers found of five proving the method, Lucien's bracers he would have thought of, his one flaw.

## Letter

$N,

A brief note, as I am in the middle of something. I am always in the middle of something.

The bracers of rock binding have been invaluable. Two of them, at least. Servo has misplaced the other three, which is exactly why I asked you for five, which rather proves the method.

The shackles are coming along. When they are done, a mage will be able to bind elementals of a strength nobody has bound before, assuming the coefficient behaves, which it will, now that I have accounted for the sand.

Lucien tells me he made you something from the stones you had left over. I would have done so myself, of course, had I thought of it, which I did not, because I was thinking about the shackles.

Gratefully, and in a hurry,
Lotwil Veriatus
