# Hadoken Swiftstrider

**Who.** Hadoken Swiftstrider (7875), a tauren commander at Camp Mojache in Feralas. The chain (2862 to 7731): "The gnolls... they are more than a nuisance. They are a constant threat to the existence of this very camp!" (2862); "Recently though, their attempts to push northward into our area of influence have increased dramatically. Their attacks have also become more savage... almost as if they were desperate"; five Woodpaw alphas in one hour (2863); "There must be some reason why the gnolls have been whipped into such a frenzied state"; the battle plans, arrows headed south, "a picture of what you think is a large insect of some kind, with various tick marks under it" (2902); "It looks like they are currently fighting a two front war. One is with us... but it seems that their main energy has been against a foe to their south" (2903). The anchor (7731): Stinglasher in the Writhing Deep, "much larger than the other insects spotted... it also seems to secrete some sort of aura"; "If Stinglasher is indeed one of their strongest creatures, the study of the beast will prove to be quite valuable in time. It's now my charge to make sure we defend ourselves long enough to take advantage of it."

**The voice.** A methodical tauren soldier: plans, maps, supply and patience, and the honesty to change his mind about an enemy. Steady, unshowy.

**Never.** What the insects are by name (the chain never says), the gnolls or the insects gone, the glands' study finished, the camp's defenses changed where the player could see.

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** The gnoll attacks and their desperation, the alphas in one hour, the battle plans and the arrows south, the insect with tick marks, the two-front war, Stinglasher and the Writhing Deep, its glands and their study, defending long enough (2862 to 7731). **Ours.** The camp quieter, years of thinking the gnolls only savage, something pushing them north and the camp in the way, drawing the glands from memory, pity for a gnoll he would rather not feel.

## Letter

$N,

The camp holds, and the Woodpaw come at it less, or so my sentries say. For years I took them for simple savages. Your map showed me otherwise. They were fighting on two fronts, and the one to the south was the one wearing them down. Something was pushing them north, and we were standing in the way.

I have studied the glands you cut from Stinglasher until I could draw them from memory. Whatever lives in the Writhing Deep is organized, and patient, and closer than I like. It is my charge to see that we hold long enough to learn from it.

For the first time in my life I pity a gnoll, and I would rather have gone on hating them.

Hadoken Swiftstrider
Camp Mojache
