# Witch Doctor Uzer'i

**Who.** Witch Doctor Uzer'i (8115), a troll at Camp Mojache in Feralas, an experimenter. The chain (3121, 3122, 3128): "Neeru Fireblade in Orgrimmar has what I need to get started here! He has mixed up a concoction that I need for my... well, you'll see. Off with you now -- to Orgrimmar. Here, take this with you. It's a shrunken head that I promised Neeru in exchange for the herbs. It's the head of some poor dwarf I ventured across in the forest" (3121); Neeru: "Please let him know that I am satisfied with the shrunken head" (3122); "This is just what I need to prepare the muisek vessel, $N. Soon we will see if my latest incantations prove to be as powerful as I had hoped" (3122). The anchor (3128): "I'll need a splintered log from a treant of Feralas. Encrusted minerals, these can be found on the mountain giants and faerie dragons to the west. Also, find me some resilient sinew from the owlbeasts of the Hinterlands or hippogryphs of Feralas. Finally, I'll need a large amount of metallic fragments"; "Excellent! These materials, combined with the muisek, should yield just the results we've been wanting." The muisek's other quests run alongside and aren't required: the letter names only the vessel and these materials.

**The voice.** A gleeful troll witch doctor: "mon", cackling ("Hah!"), proud of his craft, a little sinister, keeping his secrets for the pleasure of it. The shrunken dwarf is a trade good to him.

**Never.** What the vessel makes (unknown, later), the muisek hunts (not required), Neeru's secret (Uzer'i knows him as a supplier).

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** Neeru Fireblade's herbs for the shrunken dwarf head, Neeru satisfied, the muisek vessel, the incantations, the splintered log of a treant, encrusted minerals of giants and faerie dragons, sinew of owlbeasts or hippogryphs, metallic fragments, the results wanted (3121 to 3128). **Ours.** Every piece sorted by smell, a good customer who asks no questions and pays in herbs, the results his business and the spirits', good hands for gathering.

## Letter

$N,

Hah! The vessel is ready, mon, and the materials you brought are better than I asked for. Splintered treant, encrusted minerals, resilient sinew, all those little metal fragments. I sorted every piece myself, by smell.

Neeru says he was satisfied with the head. A good customer, that one. He asks no questions, and he pays in herbs.

What the muisek and the vessel make together is my business, and the spirits'. But I will tell you this much. When I put the last fragment in, the whole hut went quiet, the way a jungle goes quiet when something big is listening.

You have good hands for gathering, $N, and a witch doctor always notices hands.

Witch Doctor Uzer'i
Camp Mojache
