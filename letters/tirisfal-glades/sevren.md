# Magistrate Sevren

**Who.** Magistrate Sevren (1499), the Forsaken magistrate of Brill. The player cleared Rot Hide gnolls from the mass graves near Garren's Haunt, dug for "the...impressive...number of deaths Tirisfal suffered when the Plague first came", whose dead "have so far been spared an undeath", and brought back their Embalming Ichor for the Apothecaries (358: "Expertly done... It may have secrets we can use against them"). Then he wanted "a fresh report" from Deathguard Linnea (1495) at the waystation, on whether the Scourge had slipped past The Bulwark (359), and the player carried it back (360, the anchor): "It is troubling to hear of the Scourge slipping past the Bulwark. The Lich King and his minions are ceaseless in their assaults. But we will not give in. He will not take us again!"

**The voice.** A magistrate of the dead: formal, procedural, sentences built like minutes, with a Forsaken's dry wit in the pauses (the game's "impressive...number"). Under the forms, one line of the defiance the game gives him. Not Nathanos: Sevren is courteous, and means the courtesy.

**Never.** "He" without the Lich King (16980) named first, as the game does, the Scourge held back for good (some always slip through), the graves made safe (the Rot Hides come back), a finding from the Apothecaries, a hint at more work (358's "more chances to prove your worth" stays in the game), his own life or death before (the game gives none), "the living" for a reader who may be Forsaken.

**French.** Vouvoie. His last lines are the French client's own ("Le roi-liche et ses servants lancent des assauts incessants. Mais nous n'abandonnerons pas. Il ne passera pas !"), not a translation of the English.

**The game's.** The report from Deathguard Linnea, the Bulwark holding most of the Scourge with some slipping through (359, 360), the graves by Garren's Haunt and the dead spared undeath, the Rot Hides and their ichor, the Apothecaries (358), "The Lich King and his minions are ceaseless in their assaults... He will not take us again!" (360). **Ours.** The acknowledgement of receipt, promptness remarked upon in Brill, the report filed with the others, the dead still dead, his personal interest, the Apothecaries' silence, the official thanks.

## Letter

$N,

I write to acknowledge receipt of Deathguard Linnea's report, delivered by your hand and without delay. In Brill, promptness is rare enough to be remarked upon.

The report said what she told you. The Bulwark holds the greater part of the Scourge, and some slip through. I have filed it with the others that say the same.

The graves by Garren's Haunt aren't safe, but the dead in them remain dead, which was the object. They were spared what was done to us. I find I have a personal interest in keeping it so, and your work among the Rot Hides served it. The Apothecaries have the ichor. They have told me nothing, which in my experience means they are pleased.

The Lich King and his minions are ceaseless in their assaults. He will not take us again.

With my official thanks,
Magistrate Sevren
