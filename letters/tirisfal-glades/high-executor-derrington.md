# High Executor Derrington

**Who.** High Executor Derrington (10837), a Forsaken commander at the Bulwark, on Tirisfal's edge facing the Western Plaguelands. The chain (5096, 5098, 105): "Before Andorhal, we must tackle the advancing Scarlet Crusade menace from Hearthglen... My plan is to play them off of each other by sending you to destroy the command tent they've set up; use some Flame in a Bottle... Once razed, plant this Scourge banner by the tent. With some luck, they'll ignore us and focus on the Scourge for vengeance" (5096); the beacon torch on Andorhal's four watchtowers, "My priests are reporting that our beacon trackers are picking up extremely strong signals on all four towers! Dare I say it, but I believe you've executed another mission with complete and total success!" (5098). The anchor (105): "Alas, the time to attack Andorhal and drive out the lich that controls it is upon us!... Destroy Araj, $N, and bring me a shard from his phylactery as proof!"; "You've done it! Araj... the lich is finally defeated! You've done a tremendous service to the Horde on this day, $N... Please - accept this as a token for all to see and know that you have delivered the Horde a victory for the ages!"

**The voice.** An exuberant schemer: delighted by a plan that works, theatrical, generous with praise, the opposite of Hadrec's cold count. "Dare I say it."

**Never.** Araj or the Scourge gone from Andorhal (Araj comes back), the reward the player chose, the Crusade beaten, Sevren's Brill (the same glades, another voice).

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** The Crusade played off against the Scourge, Flame in a Bottle, the Scourge banner, the beacon torch and the four towers, the priests' trackers, "dare I say it", Araj the lich and his phylactery, a victory for the ages (5096 to 105). **Ours.** Saying it to everyone at the Bulwark, three plans that worked, beacons on a map, the one part no plan of his can claim.

## Letter

$N,

Dare I say it again? A triumph! I have said it to everyone at the Bulwark who would stand still for it.

I do enjoy a plan that works, and ours worked three times over. I hear the Crusade found our banner and went straight at the Scourge, exactly as hoped. The towers lit up on my priests' trackers like beacons on a map. And then you walked into Andorhal and brought me a shard of Araj's phylactery, which is the one part no plan of mine can take the credit for.

The Scourge will not give up Andorhal for one fallen lich, and I would not insult you by pretending they will. But they will remember who set the Crusade on them, who marked their towers, and who walked in after.

High Executor Derrington
The Bulwark
