# Madame Eva

**Who.** Madame Eva (265), the seer of Darkshire in Duskwood, a woman. The chain (66 to 98, The Legend of Stalvan): "Last night a horrible disturbance rippled through my veins. I sensed that my granddaughter, Alyssa, was in great danger. I consulted the cards and Death stared up at me from the table... A name came to me, the name of Stalvan" (66). Then the long trail of Stalvan Mistmantle: Clerk Daltry's registry, the Moonbrook Schoolhouse, the Lion's Pride Inn in Goldshire, the Canal District in Stormwind, the old estate and its massacre, Marshal Haggard, Tavernkeep Smitts, Commander Ebonlocke, the handwriting that matched (67 to 97). The anchor (98): "I know, $N: Stalvan is dead. I sensed a wave of hope ripple through the tainted forest. Once and for all, Duskwood is free from his blood thirst. My joy is hampered by thoughts of those who fell prey to the horrible beast." Alyssa Eva (1673) is her granddaughter by name.

**The voice.** A seer and a grandmother: the cards, the senses in her veins, tea, a warm and slightly theatrical calm. She feels things before she knows them and says so. Sorry for what her sight sent the player into.

**Never.** Stalvan gone for good (the game says "once and for all" and brings him back: the letter doesn't), Tilloa or the estate's victims named (the chain's names are the quests' own, the letter needs none), Alyssa told, a reading of the player's future (nothing to wait for).

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** The premonition, Alyssa in danger, the cards and Death on the table, the name in the trance, the trail from the registry to Moonbrook, Goldshire and Stormwind, the wave of hope through the tainted forest, the joy hampered by those who fell prey (66 to 98). **Ours.** The cards laid again and a plain card come up, the cold tea, sorry for what was found, one shadow less on her house, Alyssa never to know.

## Letter

$N,

I laid the cards for Alyssa again. Death did not come up. What came up was a card I had not seen in years, a plain one, of no importance at all. It means an ordinary day. I sat and looked at it until my tea went cold.

You followed a name out of my trance to the registry, to Moonbrook and Goldshire and Stormwind and back again, and every door you knocked on opened onto something older and worse. I sent you down that road. I am sorry for some of what you found at the end of it.

The forest is still dark. The cards say so, and they are rarely wrong about Duskwood. But there is one shadow less over this house.

Alyssa does not know how near it came, and I mean to keep it that way.

Madame Eva
