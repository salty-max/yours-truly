# Dalar Dawnweaver

**Who.** Dalar Dawnweaver (1938), a Forsaken mage at the Sepulcher in Silverpine Forest, serving Varimathras's advance for the Dark Lady. The chain (421 to 99, Arugal's Folly): "that fool, Arugal -- charlatan of Dalaran and now cursed beast of Shadowfang Keep -- let his reckless magic wreak havoc"; "How Arugal gained acceptance within the Kirin Tor is beyond me. His spell-casting knowledge seemed as transparent as a blown glass bauble" (422); the Remedy of Arugal from the wheat farm; "The old hack was not qualified to clean chamber pots in Dalaran" and the shackles of the Moonrage, "I hope I can acquire enough energy from such a limited sample. Perhaps I should have had you get more shackles" (423); "It will indeed take me longer than I had thought" and Grimson the Pale's head for the Deep Elem Mine (424); the anchor (99): Pyrewood Village, "By day, the peasants appear to be Human. But when the sun goes down the townsfolk turn into Moonrage Worgen", six Pyrewood Shackles, "Your persistence is commendable... Arugal will fall and his reckless magic shall be undone. Cheers to you, $N."

**The voice.** A scholar's contempt: precise, superior, a little petty, more interested in Arugal's bad magic than in anyone's suffering. Complains about his samples. Generous to the player in his own clipped way ("Cheers to you").

**Never.** Arugal fallen (he stays in Shadowfang Keep), the research finished (it takes longer), Pyrewood cured, a past in the Kirin Tor invented for Dalar, an errand for more shackles.

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** Arugal the fool and charlatan of Dalaran, the Kirin Tor's acceptance, the blown glass bauble, the remedy, the limited sample and more shackles, longer than he thought, Pyrewood by day and by night, cheers to you (421 to 99). **Ours.** The energy drawn and not enough, the remedy mostly understood and Arugal thought less of, a whole village on an apprentice's spell, his one talent.

## Letter

$N,

I have drawn what I could from the Pyrewood shackles you brought me. It was not enough. I suspected as much, and I believe I said so. A limited sample is a limited sample.

Still, I understand the remedy now, or most of it, and the more I understand it, the less I think of the man who cast the curse. The Kirin Tor let him into their halls. I have given up wondering why.

In Pyrewood they are still men and women by day, and still something else when the sun goes down. Arugal did that to a whole village with a spell any apprentice would be ashamed to sign. That is what I forgive least. Not the curse. The workmanship.

It will take longer than I thought. Everything to do with Arugal takes longer than I think. It may be his one talent.

Cheers to you, $N.

Dalar Dawnweaver
The Sepulcher
