# High Executor Hadrec

**Who.** High Executor Hadrec (1952), who commands the Sepulcher in Silverpine Forest. The chain (437 to 442): Nightlash and the rot hides at the Dead Fields ("The Scourge have lost ground this day. Your value to the Forsaken grows"), the Decrepit Ferry and its wrapped corpses, Deliah's ring ("The Rot Hide Gnolls must be taking those corpses to Fenris Isle!"), the ichor to Apothecary Renferrel, Bethor Iceshard naming Thule Ravenclaw, his old colleague who allied with the Scourge, the scroll back to Renferrel, a report to Hadrec and something from the arsenal (437 to 448). The anchor (442): "Now is the time to assault the Scourge's place of strength in Silverpine: Fenris Isle... destroy Thule Ravenclaw. I want his head! This mission is as dangerous as it is vital, $N. You must not fail." And: "The death of Thule Ravenclaw was no small event, $N. Renferrel tells me that the energies released when you destroyed Thule were felt even as far as the Sepulcher. Your actions ring through the ranks of the Forsaken. Your value to us grows."

**The voice.** A commander's: cold, exact, ready for the worst and saying so. Ground won and lost is how he counts. A dry edge with his own officers. Unlike Sevren, no officialese and no graves: he plans, he doesn't file.

**Never.** Thule gone for good or the island cleared (the rot hides still hold it), the Forsaken's own graves (Sevren's letter has them), the player's pick from the arsenal, Varimathras or the Dark Lady quoted, an errand.

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** Fenris Isle, Thule's head, as dangerous as it is vital, you must not fail, Renferrel and the energies felt as far as the Sepulcher, the Scourge losing ground, your value to us grows (437 to 442). **Ours.** The two reports written before the assault and the one burned, feeling it himself before anyone told him, one of ours.

## Letter

$N,

Before you left for Fenris Isle, I wrote two reports for the Undercity. One said that Thule Ravenclaw was dead. The other said that I had sent you after him and you had not come back. A commander should be ready for either. I burned the second the day you came back with his head.

Renferrel tells me the energies released when Thule fell were felt as far as the Sepulcher. He is right. I felt it myself, and I knew what you had done before anyone told me.

The Scourge are not finished in Silverpine. The Rot Hides still hold the island, and they will not leave it for one dead mage. But they have lost ground, and they lost it to one of ours.

Your value to us grows.

High Executor Hadrec
The Sepulcher
