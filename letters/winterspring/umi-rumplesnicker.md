## Letter

$N,

For as long as I've known them, my friends have been the clever ones. Legacki, Sprinkle and her cooking, Quixxil and whatever it is Quixxil does. Mine were the ideas that got laughed at, so I kept them in my pocket.

Not any more! The yeti is home again, a little scorched in one ear, and every time I look at it I think: I made that. A whole yeti. From a drawing.

They all wrote back, by the way. I won't tell you what they said, since you were there for most of it. But two of them asked how it works, and that is the part I keep reading over.

You carried a mechanical yeti halfway across the world for me, $N. Nobody has ever done anything that silly for my sake. Thank you.

Umi Rumplesnicker
Everlook
