# Chief Engineer Hinderweir VII

**Who.** Chief Engineer Hinderweir VII (1093), keeper of the Stonewrought Dam in Loch Modan, a dwarf. The chain (250 to 283, A Dark Threat Looms): with the Thandol Span destroyed and Dun Modr pillaged, he fears the Dark Irons will strike the Dam next, and "Most of the security detail here was reassigned to the Alliance Front, including my top inspector." The player finds a barrel of strange powder, takes it to Ashlan Stonesmirk in Dun Modr (deaf from blasts), who names it Seaforium, "harmless enough. But once it's wet it could blow Ironforge out of the mountain", gathers the disarming components, and stirs the mixture into the keg the Dark Iron Insurgents planted at the base of the Dam. The anchor (283): "Thank you so much, $N!! You have saved the Stonewrought Dam!... The Dam is once again safe. . . for the time being at least. . . ."

**The voice.** A nervous engineer with a big job and too few hands: exclamations, double marks ("Oh heavens!", "Time is of the essence!!"), thanking the stars, working things out on paper and wishing he hadn't. Precise about the Dam, anxious about everything else. Warm under the nerves.

**Never.** The Dark Irons beaten for good (they come back), a guard or a new watch the player could look for at the Dam, Ashlan's deafness as the joke, a word against Ironforge.

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** The Dam, the Seaforium and what it does wet, the security detail and his top inspector at the Alliance Front, the keg at the base and the mixture stirred in, "for the time being at least", thanking the stars (250 to 283). **Ours.** Looking at the base morning and evening, the sums he stopped doing, knocking on every barrel, the soup, the haste.

## Letter

$N,

The Dam still stands! I go down to the base every morning and every evening to look, and it still stands, and I thank the stars each time. Oh heavens, I hope you never learn what that powder would have done wet. I worked it out on paper twice, and then I stopped working it out!!

The security detail is still at the Alliance Front, and my best inspector with them. So it is me, whoever the mountaineers can spare, and the water. I knock on every barrel I pass now. Every single one.

You came when no one else did, and you stirred that keg as if it were soup. I don't know whether that was courage or ignorance, and I am grateful for either!

The Dam is safe, for the time being at least, and so is everyone below it.

Yours in some haste,
Hinderweir VII
Chief Engineer, Stonewrought Dam
