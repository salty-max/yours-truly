# Mull Thunderhorn

**Who.** Mull Thunderhorn (2948), at Bloodhoof Village, who made the cleansing totems when goblins "tainted our sacred water wells". For each well "the land must offer its aid": prairie wolf paws and plainstrider talons for the Winterhoof Water Well (748, 754: "already the beasts near the well drink from it eagerly"), prairie stalker and flatland cougar claws for the Thunderhorn Water Well (756, 758: "Ancestors of the Thunderhorn clan spoke to me in a dream, praising you"), prairie wolf alpha teeth for the Wildmane Water Well (759: "I can feel the sacrifice of the land in this offering, and my spirit swells with sadness, and pride"). The anchor (760): the last well cleansed, "You have cleaned the wells, and healed our land... May the wind whisper your deeds for a hundred seasons, and may the waters of Mulgore remain clean forever."

**The voice.** Earnest and ceremonial, a maker of totems who speaks of the land as a giver and of the beasts as owed thanks. Blessings come to him naturally, and he means each one. Long, rolling sentences, unhurried. Hawkwind's warmth is a family's, Mull's is the land's: no household, no chatter.

**Never.** Domestic news (Hawkwind's ground), the goblins driven off (they still guard the wells), a lesson spelled out, Thunder Bluff named (Forever's French name for area 1638 is unsettled), a walk to fetch water (Hawkwind's mother does that), a night vigil (Gadrin's).

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** The tainted wells and the three cleansings, the beasts that gave paws, talons, claws and teeth (748 to 760), the beasts drinking at Winterhoof (754), his spirit's sadness and pride (759), the spirits speaking of the player's deeds (760), the wind and the blessing "May the spirits guide you" (758), "young $N" (760). **Ours.** Watching the beasts drink, the order they come in, the thought that they drink what their kind helped make pure, the land knowing it, the wind from the north.

## Letter

Young $N,

The beasts drink at the Winterhoof well again, as eagerly as on the day it was cleansed. I have watched the plainstriders bend to it, and the wolves come down to it after them, and each time I have thought that they drink from water their own kind helped to make pure. They do not know it, but the land does, and so do I.

Every totem asked something of the beasts of Mulgore, down to the alphas' teeth, and my spirit is heavy for them still, and proud. They did not choose to give, and it was you who carried their gift to the wells.

When the wind comes down from the north, I hear the spirits speak of you.

May the spirits guide you,
Mull Thunderhorn
