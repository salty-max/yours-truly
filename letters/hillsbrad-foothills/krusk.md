# Krusk

**Who.** Krusk (2229), an orc warrior in Tarren Mill, in the Hillsbrad Foothills. The chain (498 to 508): "Me and some other warriors were sent by the Warchief to retrieve an object from Durnholde and to investigate this so-called Syndicate... They took a few of us captive, but I was able to escape" (498); Drull and Tog'thar freed from Durnholde, "So the humans thought to turn our warriors into slaves" (498); the Syndicate's papers, Gol'dir found held in Alterac, "forcing me to work, hauling burdens, digging" (533, 503); Gol'dir's word of Lord Aliden Perenolde, who meant to take orcs as slaves "just as his father and his mentor, the despised Blackmoore, had planned", and his mistress Elysa with the pendant the Warchief desires, "I will make my own way, after I have settled my business here" (506); Perenolde killed, Elysa: "please don't hurt me! I never meant your people any harm, he's had me against my will!" (507), the pendant given up, "I want no memory of him" (508). The anchor (508): "It is strange that the Warchief would expend so much over such a small thing. I do not claim to understand him, but I will follow him, whatever his commands. You did well, $N, no doubt the Warchief will be glad to hear of your success."

**The voice.** A plain orc warrior: loyal, short sentences, puzzled by the errand and at peace with being puzzled. Fair to the girl, hard on the slavers.

**Never.** Taretha's story or why Thrall wants the pendant (the chain never tells it), Thrall's reply, Gol'dir said home (he makes his own way), the Syndicate gone.

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** The Warchief's errand to Durnholde, the captured warriors, the Syndicate and slaves, Gol'dir forced to haul and dig, Perenolde and Blackmoore's legacy, Elysa held against her will, the pendant, so much over such a small thing, following the Warchief without understanding (498 to 508). **Ours.** Not understanding so small a thing, sending it on sewn into a rider's pack, Gol'dir an orc of his word, hoping the girl is far from the manor and unhurt.

## Letter

$N,

I do not understand why the Warchief wants so small a thing. A little chain, a little stone. I have seen warriors die for less, and for more, and I do not know which this is. I sent it on to Orgrimmar all the same, sewn into a rider's pack. I follow him. That has been enough for me so far, and it is enough now.

Gol'dir told you he would make his own way once his business was settled, and I believe him. He is an orc of his word.

As for the girl, I hope she has gone somewhere far from that manor. She was held there against her will, as our warriors were held at Durnholde. I am glad no one hurt her.

Krusk
Tarren Mill
