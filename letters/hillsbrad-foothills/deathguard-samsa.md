# Deathguard Samsa

**Who.** Deathguard Samsa (2418), an old Deathguard on guard in Tarren Mill in the Hillsbrad Foothills. The chain (527, 546): High Executor Darthalia's orders, "Stand at attention while addressing me, $c... quell the human infestation", the northernmost farms of Hillsbrad Fields raided, Farmer Ray and Farmer Getz executed ("My sources tell me you performed well on the Hillsbrad front"). The anchor, Souvenirs of Death (546): "I hear you were enlisted by Darthalia to wage war on the humans of Hillsbrad. So jealous I am... While you're off having all the fun -- slaying humans, pillaging the town, terrorizing innocent people -- I am stuck standing guard here in Tarren Mill. Perhaps you'll take pity on an old Deathguard like myself? You see I am collecting human skulls." Thirty of them. Waiting: "So the lucky $N returns? How many humans have you slaughtered today? Hundreds I do hope!" The reward: "Precious skulls! So beautiful! I owe you as promised, $N!" Funny and grim.

**The voice.** A cheerful ghoul of a collector: gushing, jealous, bored stiff at his post, delighted by the macabre and entirely without shame. The joke is his sincerity. Fond of the player, his supplier.

**Never.** The skulls on display anywhere the player could look (kept out of sight), a debt still open (Gazlowe's letter is the one that owes), a call for more skulls, Darthalia's remarks quoted as the joke (she has her own way of speaking in the game, the letter only says she minds).

**French.** Vouvoie. "Garde noir" is the game's title.

**The game's.** Tarren Mill, the old Deathguard stuck on guard, jealous of the fun, Darthalia's war on Hillsbrad, the thirty skulls, precious and beautiful, the lucky $N (527, 546). **Ours.** Counting them at every change of the guard, very well behaved, kept out of Darthalia's sight, guarding them most carefully, nothing ever happening, falling to pieces at his age, every one beautiful, hoping the player stops at the gate.

## Letter

Lucky $N,

Thirty! Thirty precious skulls, and every one of them beautiful! I count them at every change of the guard, in case one has rolled off somewhere. None has. They are very well behaved.

I keep them out of sight, where Darthalia can't see them. She feels a Deathguard on duty ought to be guarding. I am. I guard them most carefully.

Nothing ever happens at this post. While you were out in Hillsbrad having the time of your life, I was so jealous I could have fallen to pieces, which at my age is a real risk.

I do hope you will stop at the gate when you pass, if only so I can thank you again.

Your devoted Deathguard,
Samsa
Tarren Mill
