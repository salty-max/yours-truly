# Magistrate Henry Maleb

**Who.** Magistrate Henry Maleb (2276), magistrate of Southshore in the Hillsbrad Foothills, a man. The chain (511 to 537): an encrypted letter found among the Syndicate's tents in the Alterac foothills (511), Loremaster Dibbs in Southshore ("I have never before seen this form of encryption"), Prospector Stormpike in Ironforge who decrypts it (514, 525): "You should take this to the magistrate of Southshore... his is the closest town to Alterac; if a threat is brewing, then he should know" (525). Maleb: "This was not written by a thief, or even a noble of Alterac. It appears The Syndicate has ties to another power, and I shudder to think what that might be" (525). The anchor, Dark Council (537): "a being who calls himself Nagaz, a member of the Argus Wake. I don't know what this Argus Wake is, but if they're in alliance with the Syndicate then they must be a threat... Search in the Uplands"; the Argus Shadow Mages killed: "Well done, $N. The Argus Wake does not sound like a group we want running the Syndicate. And although you may have destroyed their leader here, Nagaz was merely the servant of another. The Argus Wake might run deep through the continent of Azeroth. Or even the world." An encrypted letter began it.

**The voice.** A small-town magistrate out of his depth and honest about it: plain, a little rueful, decent. Solomon writes like a judge, Sevren like a clerk: Maleb writes like a man who settles quarrels over nets and has been handed something far bigger.

**Never.** What the Argus Wake is (he doesn't know), whom Nagaz served (later), the Syndicate gone, Phin's warmth (the same town, another voice), an errand to look further.

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** The encrypted letter from the Syndicate's camp, Dibbs, Stormpike, the closest town to Alterac, not a thief nor a noble of Alterac, Nagaz and the Argus Wake, the Uplands, Nagaz a servant of another, running deep through Azeroth or the world (511 to 537). **Ours.** Nets, boundary stones and the drunk at the wrong door, Dibbs's books searched and still searching, liking to know where his worries live, looking north instead of at the water, better him than no one.

## Letter

$N,

I'm the magistrate of a fishing town. I settle quarrels over nets and boundary stones, and now and then a drunk who has mistaken someone else's door for his own. I wasn't made for names like the Argus Wake.

Dibbs has been through every book he owns looking for it, and found nothing.

You dealt with the mages in the Uplands, and the Syndicate is the poorer for it. I wish that were the end. But whoever Nagaz served is still out there, further off than Alterac, and I am a man who likes to know where his worries live.

So I look north in the evenings, where I used to look at the water. Thank you for bringing me what I did not want to know. Better me than no one.

Magistrate Henry Maleb
Southshore
