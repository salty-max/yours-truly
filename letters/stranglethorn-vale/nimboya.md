# Nimboya

**Who.** Nimboya (2497), a Darkspear troll at the Grom'gol Base Camp in Stranglethorn Vale. The chain (581 to 592): "A doom on my chief's youngest son, Yenniku. As is a custom with our people, he was given to the Gurubashi Trolls of Zul'Gurub, but after my tribe, the Darkspear tribe, left with the orc Thrall, poor Yenniku was lost to us. I was sent from distant Orgrimmar, back to Stranglethorn, to search for my chief's son" (581); Bloodscalp tusks, shrunken heads, "Yenniku was not among the heads, and I thank the Great Serpent for it" (582, 584); the heads of Gan'zulah and Nezzliok in the bubbling cauldron, made to talk (584 to 586); Nezzliok: "Young Yenniku is lost to his clan. He was taken by the witchdoctor Zanzil the Outcast. Now Zanzil controls him, body and soul" (588); Kin'weelay, the singing crystals, the Mind's Eye taken from Mai'Zoth and reshaped into a soul gem (589 to 591). The anchor (592): Yenniku stunned and his soul taken into the gem at the Ruins of Aboraz; Nimboya: "You've done it! You freed Yenniku from the will of Zanzil, and completed the mission I had thought was so desperate. Our tribe will remember your name in songs, $N, and we will etch your tale in stone so that it will live forever." The plan named 586 (Speaking with Gan'zulah) as the anchor: Yenniku's story ends at 592, the quest the plan's row means, 23 October 2026.

**The voice.** A Darkspear envoy far from home: grave, devout (the Great Serpent), plain words with a troll's rhythm, joy that hurts. Bringing a son home in a stone.

**Never.** Yenniku's body or what becomes of him after (later, unknown), Zanzil or Gan'zulah gone for good, the chief named, Trol'kalar (Zengu's chain, though the sword comes to him), the stone already cut (a promise kept off-screen, not told).

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** The chief's youngest son, given to the Gurubashi by custom, lost when the Darkspear left with Thrall, sent from Orgrimmar, the heads, the Great Serpent, Zanzil's hold on him, the soul gem, freed from Zanzil's will, the songs and the stone (581 to 592). **Ours.** Carrying him home himself, a son in a stone no bigger than a fist, counting Bloodscalp heads, patience across a whole sea.

## Letter

$N,

The chief sent me across the sea to find his youngest son, and I will carry him home myself. Not as he left. In a gem no bigger than my fist, himself, free of Zanzil at last. It is more than I dared hope when I was counting Bloodscalp heads by the cauldron.

The Great Serpent spared him the headhunter's axe, and you spared him the rest. A soul held so long by another will not wake all at once. Darkspear know patience. We crossed a whole sea once, and came back for him.

Our songs will carry your name, mon. The stone I will leave to the carvers.

Nimboya
Grom'gol Base Camp
