# Crank Fizzlebub

**Who.** Crank Fizzlebub (2498), a goblin in Booty Bay in Stranglethorn Vale, a dealer in useful substances. The chain (1111 to 1127): Kravel Koalbeard's errands on the Shimmering Flats, the parts, the rumors about Nazz Steamboil, Krazek's dream dust (1111 to 1117); Kravel: "$N, you're a $c I can trust, both to get the job done and to keep your mouth shut while doing it. I've been brewing up a new scheme" (1118); Crank: "I see. Kravel has a big scheme planned. He has a sinister mind -- I like that about him. So you'll need something to addle wits, eh? Something powerful... I wonder if the mixture Zanzil feeds his zombies could be used..." (1118); Zanzil's mixture, "Ah, very good! Here is your pay. And here is a little something extra... for your discretion" (621); "I figured out how to use Zanzil's mixture with stout to make a powerful libation. A Fool's Stout... Zanzil's mixture has a weird effect on the stout's yeast, kicking it into high gear. So it won't stay fresh forever" (1119); the pit crews made drunk, Kravel's bet on the next race (1120 to 1122). The anchor (1127): more of the mixture, more stout, "Here is your pay. And here is a little something extra... for your discretion."

**The voice.** A sly goblin fixer: confidential, smooth, amused by schemes, talks around things. Unlike Gazlowe (blunt boss) and Razzeric (all speed), he whispers and enjoys it.

**Never.** The race's result, Nazz's secret told, Zanzil named as more than his mixture's source, an invitation to bring more (the game keeps the quest, the letter doesn't ask), Gazlowe's "rarer than gold".

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** Kravel's sinister scheme, keeping your mouth shut, Zanzil's mixture, the Fool's Stout, the yeast kicked into high gear, not fresh forever, the pit crews, the bet, the pay and a little something extra for your discretion (1111 to 1127). **Ours.** A note between friends, drinks owed him in Booty Bay, strange things to yeast and to people, never asking what the scheme was for, the letter as the extra.

## Letter

$N,

Between friends, then.

The last batch went out to the flats, and I am told the pit crews were very quiet for a day or two afterwards. Meanwhile a good many people in Booty Bay seem to owe me a drink, and I have stopped paying for my own.

Zanzil's mixture does strange things to yeast. Strange things to people, too, but yeast is my business, and I would keep it that way if I were you.

Here is what I like about you. You carried a sinister scheme from Booty Bay to the flats and back, and not once did you ask what it was for. I paid you a little extra for that at the time. This letter is not payment. Think of it as a compliment, from a man who does not give them away.

Crank Fizzlebub
Booty Bay
