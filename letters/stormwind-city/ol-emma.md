# Ol' Emma

**Who.** Ol' Emma (3520), who wanders Stormwind City talking to herself, an old woman, born Emma Felstone. The chain (5021, 5022, 5048, 5050, 5051): Janice Felstone's ghost on the Felstone Farm in the Western Plaguelands, "The package must be delivered... delivered before I'm trapped here!" (5021); the package in the barn, "addressed in a hurried hand to an Emma Felstone of Stormwind; the delivery of the package is dated for well over four years ago" (5022); the census: "She's a tad ornery, and quite possibly out of her gourd... she doesn't hurt anything or anyone, so folks pretty much leave her be" (5048). Emma: "My name is indeed Emma Felstone - you're the first person in years to call me that. Who... who are you? You say you knew... my sister?!... When the plague broke out in Lordaeron, I had come to expect the worst. So many of my family died... or worse. Here I am now, crazy Ol' Emma, all alone. What are you, a grave-robber or something?" (5048); then: "It would seem that I was wrong about you $N, and for that I owe you an apology. My sister sent me a number of personal effects, including humorously enough the deed to the Felstone farm. I think I'll pass on reclaiming it right now... It is half of a good luck charm... Maybe her knowing I have these things will now put her soul to rest" (5050); the anchor (5051): the charm made whole and Janice freed, which Emma never sees. The letter needs the Alliance's road (5048) and the end (5051): Jeremiah Payson writes the Horde's (the plan's two writers for one parcel).

**The voice.** Ornery, sharp, lonely, funnier than she lets on: short sentences, an old woman's bluntness, a little scattered and entirely sane on paper. Signs her own name, the one nobody uses.

**Never.** Janice's last words or the cold (Emma wasn't there), the ghost's rest stated as fact (she hopes), the Plaguelands farm visited, Jeremiah (another letter, another side), the plague's horrors told.

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** Emma Felstone, the first person in years to use the name, the sister, the plague and the family lost, crazy Ol' Emma alone, the grave-robber and the apology, the personal effects, the deed to the farm and passing on it, the half-charm, her soul at rest maybe (5021 to 5051). **Ours.** Signing the name again like a coat left somewhere, Janice's packing, the recipe of their mother's, sorry said once, hoping and not wanting to be told otherwise.

## Letter

$N,

Emma Felstone writing. I've taken to signing it again, after all these years. It feels like a coat I left at somebody's house.

I've been through everything Janice sent. Old letters. A recipe of Mother's I'd forgotten. The deed to the farm, of all things, as if I'd ever go back there. She always did pack as though she was moving in for good.

I called you a grave-robber. I'll say I'm sorry once, plain, and then we'll not speak of it again. I'm sorry.

If that charm did what I hoped, then she's resting, and not waiting on a delivery any more. If it didn't, don't tell me. I'd rather think it did.

Emma Felstone
