# Baros Alexston

**Who.** Baros Alexston (1646), City Architect of Stormwind, at City Hall in Cathedral Square, a man. The chain (373 to 396): Edwin VanCleef's unsent letter, found on his body, addressed to him: "I would as much expect a letter from my dead grandmother... He was a peerless fighter in his younger years... Edwin... I see the years haven't changed you a bit, an idealist as ever, and a romantic. He doesn't care who he hurts, $N. Revenge has consumed him. But then, I'm not sure I can blame him" (373). "VanCleef and I were members of the Stonemasons Guild. Our main project was to rebuild Stormwind after the War. When we had finished our duties, we were cheated. The nobles refused to pay us for our work. Some of the more senior of the Stonemasons were offered governmental jobs, but VanCleef refused it out of loyalty to all the Stonemasons. He led a riot and left the city, swearing revenge" (389). Then Bazil Thredd in the Stockade, Thredd's visitor, Shaw, Trias, Lescovar and Marzon in the castle garden (389 to 394), Shaw's advice to tell everything "but that it was your hand that slew Lescovar" (395), and Baros: "I have heard that Lord Gregor Lescovar was found dead not long ago... You have my gratitude that you have saved me, and many others, from VanCleef's revenge" (395). The anchor (396): his report to the King, delivered by the player, the King away "on a diplomatic mission".

**The voice.** An architect: measured, exact, a builder's eye on the city. Grateful and uneasy at once. He took the post VanCleef refused, and he knows it. Honest with himself in a careful way.

**Never.** Lady Prestor, any wink at what lies behind the nobles (a letter that reads the same after the reveal), the King's whereabouts, Lescovar's death laid at the player's door (Shaw kept it from him: "I have not asked how"), Vanessa VanCleef or Westfall's later story, the Unsent Letter's words quoted (the item's text, not the chain's).

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** The Stonemasons, rebuilding Stormwind after the war, the nobles who refused to pay, the posts offered and VanCleef's refusal, revenge, "he doesn't care who he hurts", "I'm not sure I can blame him", the unsent letter, the Stockade, Lescovar found dead, the report carried to the Keep (373 to 396). **Ours.** Walking past walls Edwin laid on the way to City Hall, no longer pointing them out, not asking how Lescovar died, keeping the city's plans as an answer some mornings and not others.

## Letter

$N,

I walk to City Hall every morning past walls the Stonemasons raised after the war. Edwin set some of those stones himself. I used to point them out to visitors. I have stopped.

You carried his unsent letter to me, and then followed it through the Stockade and beyond. I have heard that Lord Lescovar was found dead. I have not asked how, and I will not. I am grateful. I say it first, because the rest is harder to say.

When the nobles refused to pay us, some of us were offered posts. I took mine. Edwin refused his, out of loyalty to the others, and swore revenge. I told you he did not care who he hurt, and that I was not sure I could blame him. I have not changed my mind about either.

The city stands, and I keep its plans. Some mornings that feels like an answer to him, and some mornings it feels like the post he would not take.

Baros Alexston
City Architect
