# Elling Trias

**Who.** Elling Trias (482), Master of Cheese, at Trias' Cheese in the Trade District of Stormwind, a man, and a man with "contacts". The chain (1274 to 1267, The Missing Diplomat): Bishop DeLavey's discreet request, his old friend Jorgen ("I'll help ya, but not because I want to, but because I'm obligated... don't go blabbin' at any taverns"), Jorgen's note to Elling ("not often you get a delivery that's not a crate or barrel when all you do is work"; "Jorgen, you ol' sonuva.... I'll start using my contacts here, but you're doing most of the footwork so I save some coin"), Watcher Backus in Duskwood and the Defias docket, Dashel Stonefist in Old Town, Slim in Menethil, Elling's retired contact Mikhail the bartender ("I never thought I'd miss the excitement... Don't mention that to Elling--he'd probably try and get me to come back"), Tapoke Jahn's confession: "They took the king. Yeah, the king of Stormwind, not some lowly diplomat... The guy's name was Hendel" (1250), Mikhail sending word to Elling, then Theramore: Commander Samaul, Private Hendel subdued and taken for questioning, "we'll see about finding the King" (1264 to 1324). The anchor (1267) ends at Theramore's lady, who never writes and isn't named.

**The voice.** A spymaster who sells cheese and enjoys both: wry, thrifty, discreet. Writes his letter as a cheese order, in case other eyes read it: the king is "the wheel", the trail a supplier's. The plain sentences at the end are the only ones he means to be read plainly.

**Never.** The king named, his fate (unknown at the chain's end), Lady Prestor or any wink at who is behind it, Theramore's lady named, the Lescovar affair (another chain), Gazlowe's trade talk ("rarer than gold" is his).

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** The cheese shop in the Trade District, Jorgen's obligation and his warning about taverns, the footwork to save coin, Menethil and Mikhail who misses the excitement and mustn't say so, the king taken on his way to Theramore, Hendel taken there, finding the King still ahead (1274 to 1267). **Ours.** The letter as an order, the wheel gone from Menethil, pretending not to know what Mikhail misses, out of his hands, more footwork than he paid for, nothing heard from anyone but the player, noticing most things.

## Letter

Dear valued customer,

Regarding your recent inquiry. The wheel you were looking for left Menethil by ship well before you started asking, as you found out in Menethil. Our friend at the harbor sends his regards and says he misses the excitement, which he asked me not to repeat, so I'll pretend I didn't write it.

The rest of the order is with our friends in Theramore now, and they are careful people. Whether the wheel turns up again is out of my hands.

I will say one thing plainly. You did more of the footwork than I paid for, and not a word of it has reached my shop from anyone but you. I've noticed. I notice most things.

With thanks for your custom,
Elling Trias
Trias' Cheese, Trade District
