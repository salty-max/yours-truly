# Foreman Thazz'ril

**Who.** Foreman Thazz'ril (11378), an orc foreman in the Valley of Trials, where the peons gather lumber "but they're always taking naps!" He gave the player his Foreman's Blackjack to wake five Lazy Peons with "a good smack", then took it back: "Good, good. Maybe they'll think twice before slacking next time! Thanks for the help!" (5441, the anchor, a single quest). The first letter for orcs and trolls.

**The voice.** Gruff, loud, exasperated: short sentences barked across a work site, funny without meaning to be. Writes in a foreman's tally that keeps turning into a complaint. His thanks are a grunt, and real. The terse one of the wave.

**Never.** Cruelty past the smack (a joke, not a beating), the peons reformed (they sleep again on every realm), Thazz'ril's Pick (6394, another quest), the player's race in a sentence (no "a $R": the article breaks).

**French.** Tutoie: a foreman shouting across the yard.

**The game's.** The lumber, the napping peons, the blackjack lent and returned, "think twice before slacking", "Thanks for the help!" (5441). **Ours.** The tally, the count of sleepers, the one asleep on his feet, the peons waking at footsteps, their question and his answer.

## Letter

$N,

Lumber: short. Peons: asleep. Two under the same tree, one on the lumber pile, and one standing up. I didn't know they could do that.

They remember you, though. Word went round that you were loose in the valley with my blackjack. Now when one hears footsteps he's on his feet and swinging an axe before his eyes are open. Doesn't last. Better than nothing.

They ask if you're coming back. I tell them yes. I don't tell them when.

Good work. Thanks for the help.

Thazz'ril
Foreman
