# Master Gadrin

**Who.** Master Gadrin (3188), an elder of the Darkspear trolls at Sen'jin Village. The anchor, Zalazane (826): the witchdoctor dwells on the Echo Isles, "the isles we once called home", and sends his trolls to the mainland "to hex our people and drag more of them under his sway". The player killed his Voodoo Trolls and Hexed Trolls, "former Darkspear trolls, now lost to us", and brought back Zalazane's Head: "With Zalazane gone, our tribe can again sleep in peace. Thank you, $N. The Darkspear tribe owes you much." In Classic Era the quest stands alone. In Forever it follows Report to Sen'jin Village (805), which the letter doesn't need.

**The voice.** Grave and spare, an elder speaking for the tribe ("we", "ours"), sentences that stop where a speaker would pause. The second terse one of the wave. He gives the facts of a night, not his feelings, and the feeling is in what he chose to tell. No "mon": Gadrin never says it in the game.

**Never.** Zalazane gone for good or the isles taken back (Zalazane and his trolls come back, the isles stay his), Vol'jin or any later lore, Minshina's Skull (808, another quest), a hint at Bom'bay's cures, children or a fire (Hawkwind's).

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** Zalazane, the Echo Isles as the home they lost, the hexed as former Darkspear "lost to us", the head, the tribe sleeping in peace again (826). **Ours.** His sleepless night by the water, the drums that didn't come, the beach that was his, watching the isles, the grief the tribe keeps for itself. No thanks spelled out: the night he tells is the thanks.

## Letter

$N,

The night you brought me the head, I did not sleep. I sat by the water and listened. No drums came over from the isles. Behind me the village slept, all of it, the way it used to. I wanted to hear that.

From the shore you can see the Echo Isles. I still know which beach was mine. They are not ours. I watch them anyway.

Some of those you fought were Darkspear, before the hex. Do not grieve them. That is ours to do, and we have done it.

Gadrin
