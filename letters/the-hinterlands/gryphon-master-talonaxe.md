# Gryphon Master Talonaxe

**Who.** Gryphon Master Talonaxe (5636), a Wildhammer dwarf at Aerie Peak in the Hinterlands. The chain (2988 to 2994): "The son of our mightiest gryphon is gone! Swiftwing's heir, Sharpbeak, was lost while hunting with his parents... That gryphon is destined to lead the next generation of gryphons. If he is lost then the strength and spirit of our fleet will suffer immensely" (2988); the Witherbark cages, the Altar of Zul (2988, 2989); Thadius Grimshade the diviner, "He left Aerie Peak years ago to pursue darker knowledge, but in his heart he is still a Wildhammer!" (2990), Nekrum's medallion from Zul'Farrak, the divination (2991, 2992); Sharpbeak caged high on Jintha'Alor, Vile Priestess Hexx's key (2993). The anchor (2994): "Swiftwind and Keenclaw returned with their child moments ago, and are fussing over him like new parents. Seeing them together again is enough to give even this old dwarf mist in the eyes... The gryphons of Aerie peak, and their riders, will sing your name for a long, long time." The parents' names are in the text only: the letter doesn't use them.

**The voice.** A Wildhammer master of the aerie: proud, sentimental under the gruffness, thinks in wings and riders and songs. Solemn where Agnar laughs.

**Never.** The parents named, Sharpbeak said to lead already (he is destined to), Thadius's darker knowledge judged, Agnar's jokes (the same peak, another voice), a debt (Gazlowe's).

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** Sharpbeak, destined to lead the next generation, his parents fussing like new parents, the mist in the old dwarf's eyes, Thadius still a Wildhammer at heart, the gryphons and riders singing the player's name a long time (2988 to 2994). **Ours.** Sharpbeak's first flight with the whole wing, too far out and too proud, brought into line, word sent to Thadius, some who won't say his name, the riders' first-year song and the rhyme.

## Letter

$N,

Sharpbeak flew with the whole wing for the first time. He was too far out on the left and too proud of himself by half, and his mother brought him back into line twice. It was the finest thing I've seen from this peak in years.

I sent word to Thadius at Nethergarde, for his part in it. He went off after darker knowledge long ago, and there are some here who still won't say his name. But it was his sight that found the lad, and in his heart he's a Wildhammer yet.

The riders are making a song of it. It's not a good song. Wildhammer songs never are, the first year. They'll sing it anyway, for a long, long time, and your name's in every verse they've got so far.

Gryphon Master Talonaxe
Aerie Peak
