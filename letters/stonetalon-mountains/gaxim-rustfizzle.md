# Gaxim Rustfizzle

**Who.** Gaxim Rustfizzle (4077), a gnome of the Enlightened Assembly of Arcanology, Alchemy and Engineering Sciences, on the Webwinder Path in Stonetalon, working beside the night elf Kaela Shadowspear against the Venture Company ("Do not judge Gaxim prematurely", 1085). The chain (1085 to 1074): "it's my goal... nay, my DUTY! to show the night elves that a blend of magic, mixtures, and mechanisms can help save their forests"; the goblins held back at Windshear Crag (1071); high explosives, "don't worry, they're not as dangerous as everyone thinks"; Lomac Gearstrip (4081) in Tinker Town, who makes the NG-5 only once the player proves an alchemist ("If you're really a friend of Gaxim's, then tell him I say hello", 1072 to 1074). The anchor (1074): "NG-5 is the unequivocal component in the recipe to ignite the wagons of dynamite. What? Yes, dynamite... wagons of it. It's the perfect distraction, I tell you... just wait and see." Covert Ops (1079, 1080) follow and are not required: the letter never tells how the dynamite went.

**The voice.** A gnome inventor at full speed: big words, bigger plans, safety assurances nobody asked for, pride in the Assembly ("nay, my DUTY!"). Funny because he means every word. Plans change every time he looks at them.

**Never.** The plan's outcome (Covert Ops are later quests), the goblins driven off, an invitation to come and watch, malice toward the night elves (he wants their trust).

**French.** Tutoie: an enthusiast to his accomplice.

**The game's.** The Assembly and its mission, magic, mixtures and mechanisms, the explosives "not as dangerous as everyone thinks", Lomac and his hello, the NG-5, the wagons of dynamite, Kaela's plea not to judge him (1085 to 1074). **Ours.** The NG-5 arriving whole, Lomac the finest chemist he was shouted at by, the plan he won't tell, the night elves' look, and his duty all the same.

## Letter

$N,

The NG-5 arrived in perfect condition, which is to say it did not arrive all over you, and I count that a success for everyone involved!

Lomac is the finest chemist I have ever been shouted at by. If he said hello, he meant it. If he said anything else, he also meant it, only less.

Everything is in place. I won't tell you the plan, partly because a plan told is a plan spoiled, partly because it changes every time I look at it. It involves wagons. It involves dynamite, which is not as dangerous as everyone thinks. It involves magic, mixtures and mechanisms all working together, which is the whole point of the Assembly and, frankly, of me.

The night elves still look at me as if I might go off. That's fair. Showing them a gnome can help save their forests is my goal, nay, my DUTY!

Gaxim Rustfizzle
Enlightened Assembly of Arcanology, Alchemy and Engineering Sciences
