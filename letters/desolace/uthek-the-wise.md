# Uthek the Wise

**Who.** Uthek the Wise (5397), a shaman of the Gelkis centaurs, at the Gelkis Village in southwestern Desolace, a woman ("Through her we might strike an alliance with the Gelkis", 1382). The chain (1368 or 1382, then 1370 to 1380): "The earth speaks to me. And it says you have killed many Magram. This is good" (1368); the Magram's stores of meat, "Good. I can hear the loud Magram bellies grumble from their village, far away!" (1370); the draenethyst, "a red crystal... new to our earth. It comes from a place where mountains fly, and where demons rule", taken from Ongeku in the Swamp of Sorrows, "I can feel strong magic in it!" (1373); Khan Jehn's head, "Khan Jehn was strong, but stupid. He thought his own strength was great. He did not see that earth magic is stronger" (1374). The anchor (1380): the Maraudine War Horn in the Valley of Spears, Khan Hratha and the piece of a key; "He is dead! Khan Hratha is dead! And now the Maraudine will fall! When the Gelkis rule this land, $N, we will remember you. And the spirits of the earth will sing your name for all time." The Kolkar raid (1384) is the Alliance's road only: the letter leaves it out.

**The voice.** A centaur shaman: short, hard sentences, the earth as her witness, glee at her enemies' weakness, a little frightening. Writes as she speaks, plainly, and is pleased with herself.

**Never.** The key's purpose (later), the Maraudine fallen or Hratha gone for good (the game brings him back), the Gelkis ruling (her hope), the Kolkar charms (not every road's), Mangletooth's broken grammar (hers is plain, not broken).

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** The earth that speaks, many Magram killed, this is good, the Magram bellies grumbling, the draenethyst from where mountains fly and demons rule, her strong magic, Khan Jehn strong but stupid, earth magic stronger, Khan Hratha dead, the Maraudine to fall, the Gelkis to rule and remember, the spirits singing the player's name (1368 to 1380). **Ours.** The earth saying the player's name, still hearing the bellies, the red crystal warm at night, the Khans who did not listen.

## Letter

$N,

The earth has your name in its mouth now. It is a good name.

You brought down Khan Hratha. You brought down Khan Jehn. The Magram are already weak. I can still hear their bellies grumble when the wind is right. I laugh every time.

The red crystal sits warm in my hands at night. My magic is strong now, stronger than any Khan believed. They trusted their strength. They did not listen to the earth. The earth remembers who did not listen.

Your name is spoken at our fires already. The earth knows it, and I will be listening.

Uthek the Wise
