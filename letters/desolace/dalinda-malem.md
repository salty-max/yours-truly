# Dalinda Malem

**Who.** Dalinda Malem (5644), a night elf of House Malem, a Darnassian merchant family, a woman. The chain (1437 to 1440): Vahlarriel Demonslayer at Nijel's Point in Desolace, sent to find "two missing nobles of a Darnassian merchant family--House Malem. The father of the house fears the worst"; the wagon, the pendant, the tracks to Thunder Axe Fortress and the Burning Blade (1437, 1465); "My superiors have shown some concern over Tyranis' and his motives for coming here. It is more important to them that Dalinda is found" (1438). Dalinda, captive: "I think they mean to sacrifice me to their demon masters! I knew coming to Desolace was a mistake, but Tyranis has recently begun craving knowledge of all things arcane--even against the wishes of our people" (1438); her brother found, and dead: "This is terrible! But I suppose it was necessary. If it wasn't by your hand, it would have surely been by that of my people's. I suppose I should thank you--please forgive me if it doesn't seem sincere" (1439). The anchor (1440), her escape: "allow me to thank you now for everything you've done for me and my family"; Vahlarriel: "Dalinda made it safely back here to Nijel's Point... under her own power... the embarrassment of Tyranis' decisions is hers to endure. It is something she will have to deal with when she returns to Darnassus." The game keeps her captive in the fortress for others: the letter is written from Nijel's Point, where the game says she arrived.

**The voice.** A noble daughter, careful and courteous, grieving a brother she half-blames and half-defends. Torn, and honest about it. Gratitude that costs her something.

**Never.** Tyranis's death told in detail (the player knows), her father's reaction (it hasn't happened), Vahlarriel's superiors' judgment repeated as her own, the Burning Blade said gone, a visit to Darnassus asked.

## Letter

$N,

I am writing from Nijel's Point, with my bags packed for Darnassus. Vahlarriel says I may go when I am ready. I keep finding small reasons not to be ready yet.

I thanked you for my brother's death. I have thought about that more than about anything the orcs did to me. I meant it, and I did not mean it, and both are still true. If it had not been your hand, it would have been one of my people's. That is a cold comfort, but it is the one I have.

My father will ask what Tyranis wanted out here. I will tell him the truth: knowledge, more than was good for him, more than our people allow. I will not tell him how it ended.

You brought me out of that fortress and let me walk out on my own feet. That part I will remember best.

Dalinda Malem
