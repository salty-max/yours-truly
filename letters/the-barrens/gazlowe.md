# Gazlowe

**Who.** Gazlowe (3391), who runs Ratchet. The anchor is Stolen Booty (888): the observatory's smaller lens and the boot shipment from Drizzlik in Booty Bay, taken back from the Southsea Freebooters' camp. Before it: the pirates thinned out (887), his ledger carried to Wharfmaster Dizzywig, who is "on the payroll" (890, 892). His last line in the game: "don't forget about how well Gazlowe pays for service!" The quest pays in coin and a pick from the crate: gloves or boots.

**The voice.** Fast, friendly, businesslike. Calls the player "kid", as the game does. He knows the difference between cheap labour and someone who brings the goods back. He can sincerely thank a person and dispute the cost of the paper in the same letter. His bookkeeping jokes must make sense. He is still in your debt, on paper, and that is the joke: the books are always being fixed, never the debt (the user's call, 23 Oct 2026). Nothing the player could wait for.

**Never.** A real con, meanness, a sum the player could check against the game, a payment promised, the Vice Admiral (only a word in an English quest text: "the trade fleet").

**French.** Tutoie, "$gGamin:Gamine;".

**The game's.** The lens needed for the observatory, the recovered boots, the pay (888's coin), Dizzywig and Gazlowe's payroll (892), "how well Gazlowe pays" (888), the pirates (887 to 888, and they come back). **Ours.** The fitted lens, the customers' silence, Dizzywig's accounting and the debt it leaves, ninety-nine in a hundred, the invoice. The debt is a bookkeeping joke ("Not the difference, kid. The books."), never a payment to come, and the P.S. sends nobody to the beach.

## Letter

Kid,

The lens is fitted, the telescope works, and the boots are back on the shelves. Customers quit asking where they were. Best silence I ever paid for.

Listen. Dizzywig filed your pay under freight. I made him change it. Freight doesn't walk into a pirate camp and walk out with the right crates. By my books, that leaves me owing you the difference. So Dizzywig is fixing the books. Not the difference, kid. The books.

Everybody in Ratchet promises. Ninety-nine in a hundred come back with a story about why they couldn't. You came back with my boots. In my line, that's rarer than gold, and I'd know.

Gazlowe

P.S. The pirates are back on the beach. I've sent them an invoice.
