# Mangletooth

**Who.** Mangletooth (3430), a quilboar of the Razormane, caged in Camp Taurajo in the Barrens, who sells the Horde what he knows. The chain (878 to 889): Tribes at War, the Bristleback's spiritual leaders struck "so the Razormanes can defeat them and rule the Barrens", and his grievance, "Mangletooth aids his people, and what do they do? They turn their backs on him. They forget him and leave him to rot in cage! I aid them even from locked behind bars and they send no one to save me!"; the Blood Shards of Agamaggan, "Mangletooth may be caged, but no cage can stop Agamaggan's power! The touch of the shard, the feel of it... it has been a long time, $R. Do you know what it is like? To have your power severed from you? But, that is a tale for another time." The anchor, Spirit of the Wind (889): ten shards for a blessing of speed, "You will run with zhevra and the wind will be jealous of you. It will not last long... Go with Agamaggan's spirit as your guide, $R." Forever's text is Classic's, with the request also given as the quest's description.

**The voice.** His own: third person, broken grammar, the snort written as the game writes it, bitter and proud, cunning ("Mangletooth is no fool"). Calls the player by their race, as he does. Writes himself, badly, and says so.

**Never.** A plea to be freed (an errand), the tale of his severed power told (the game keeps it "for another time"), the Razormane said to rule the Barrens, how he sends a letter from a cage explained, a cruel joke at his cage.

**French.** Vouvoie. The snort is the French client's.

**The game's.** The cage, his people's backs turned, the Bristleback weakened, Agamaggan's power that no cage stops, the touch of the shard after a long time, power severed, the zhevra and the jealous wind, Mangletooth is no fool (878 to 889). **Ours.** Hands not made for pens, watching the player run from the cage, counting days and stopping, hoping the player never knows, grateful and no fool, both.

## Letter

$R,

Mangletooth writes. <snort> Hands not made for pens. Read it anyway.

Mangletooth saw you run, from the cage. You ran with zhevra. The wind was jealous, like Mangletooth said. <snort> Agamaggan's spirit still answers Mangletooth, through bars and all. No cage stops that. Remember.

Bristleback weaker now, thanks to you and to Mangletooth. And still the Razormane do not come. Mangletooth counted the days. Then Mangletooth stopped counting. <snort>

The touch of the shards. Long time since Mangletooth held one. You do not know what it is, to have your power cut away from you. Mangletooth hopes you never know.

Mangletooth is no fool. Mangletooth is grateful. Both. <snort>

Mangletooth
Camp Taurajo
