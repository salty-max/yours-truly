## Letter

$N,

I am no hand at letters. She was the one with words.

She held three quilboar off by herself. When more came, I led some away and told myself that was the brave part. I don't tell myself that now.

The druid who found me on the road told me to rest. I rested. Then I went back to the camp where we were separated, and I killed every quilboar I found there. That did not help either.

You found her when I couldn't. I don't know what you saw there, and I am not asking.

Thank you, $C.

Mankrik
