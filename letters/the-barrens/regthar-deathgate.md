# Regthar Deathgate

And those are the most dangerous... It is good that he is dead"), Verog the Dervish drawn out at the Stagnant Oasis ("You should be proud of your deed, $N. ## Letter

$N,

The bunkers held. Krom'zar came all the way from Desolace for his vengeance, and what he found was you.

Barak, Verog, Hezrul, and then Krom'zar. I have fought centaur most of my life, and I have never seen four of their leaders fall to one fighter. A centaur with the wit to lead is rare, and they are the dangerous ones. You took the dangerous ones.

The Kolkar will come again. They always have. When they do, they will come telling each other about you, and I hope they tell it badly.

Stand tall, $N.

Regthar Deathgate
