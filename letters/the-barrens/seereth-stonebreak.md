# Seereth Stonebreak

**Who.** Seereth Stonebreak (4049), a shaman of the Horde on the border of the Stonetalon Mountains and the Barrens, a woman. The chain (1062, 1068): "The goblin-run Venture Company moved into the Stonetalon Mountains, chopping down trees and burning huge stretches of forest. The spirits of this place are nearly blind with pain and rage... Show them in the language they best understand... Violence"; the loggers of the Greatwood Vale, "We may ultimately need the wisdom of tauren elders to calm the spirits of Stonetalon, but... Ridding the Venture Company of its personnel is a good first move." The anchor, Shredding Machines (1068): "This is why we shaman can hear the plea of the spirits of Stonetalon. Many of their whisperings are lost to me, but one message, one vision, is clear... two man-shaped machines in Windshear Crag... XT:4 and XT:9." Waiting: "The tree spirits wail at the loss of their brethren." The reward: "The destruction of the machines XT:4 and XT:9 have soothed many of the spirits of Stonetalon, $N. With their loss, let us hope the Venture Company will remove itself from these mountains. For if they do not, then I fear the atrocities this place has suffered will pale to what lies ahead."

**The voice.** Fierce and practical: a shaman who answers the spirits' pain with an axe and says so. Blunt about what's left undone. Not reverent (Mull) and not a coach (Ventaari): a fighter who hears trees.

**Never.** The Venture Company gone or the shredders gone for good (they come back), the spirits healed (many soothed), the spirits speaking of the player (Mull's), a race said for her (the game's texts don't say it), a call to come back.

**The game's.** The Venture Company, the burning, the spirits blind with pain and rage, the wailing for their brethren, the vision of XT:4 and XT:9 at Windshear Crag, many soothed, the elders' wisdom, the hope they leave and the fear if they don't, violence as the language they understand (1062, 1068). **Ours.** Most of the wailing stopped and not the grief, the spirits hurt and no longer blind, the smoke on the wind, one victory not a forest, the marks and the work divided.

## Letter

$N,

The goblins still log the Crag. On a west wind I can smell their smoke from here on the border. Two broken machines will not teach them, and I never thought they would.

But most of the wailing has stopped. Not the grief, the wailing. The spirits are hurt, still, and they have every right to be. They are no longer blind with it.

The elders may yet have to come and calm what is left. I said as much to you before. I say it again so that you never mistake one victory for a forest.

The spirits gave me the names of those machines. You gave the machines the only language the goblins understand. I think that is a fair division of the work.

In anger and in hope,
Seereth Stonebreak
