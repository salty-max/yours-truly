# Captain Garran Vimes

**Who.** Captain Garran Vimes (4944), on the top floor of Foothold Citadel on Theramore Isle in Dustwallow Marsh, a man. The chain (1282 or 1302, then 1252 to 1287): James Hyal, "Smiling Jim", who "opened an inn at the edge of the Barrens. Wanted to make a nice place for travelers to stop over... Then we lost control of the roads in the marsh... and the Shady Rest Inn was burnt to the ground" (1282, or his brother's word in the Wetlands, 1302); a badge in the rubble, Lieutenant Paval Reethe's: "It's never good news when a guard's badge is brought to me. Sometimes it's a man or woman who's fallen in battle, and I'm left to bring the news to the grieving family. Other times we never find out what happened to them. And other times it's even worse than that" (1252); Adjutant Tesoran's books, "Listed as missing" (1259, 1285); "Missing? That would be Tesoran's kind way of saying traitor. No doubt he's one of those fools so blindly loyal to Admiral Daelin that he turned traitor. Isn't that a delicious irony? The deserters accuse us of betraying the Alliance" (1285); Balos Jacken at Lost Point: Reethe thrown out for raiding the ogres of Brackenwall, "So, why don't you help us out and forget you bumped into us?" (1287). The anchor (1287): "I will send Lieutenant Caldwell to deal with the deserters at Lost Point. In addition, I will instruct him to find Reethe. The marsh is not so big and trackless that he should be unable to find him." What Caldwell finds is later quests: the letter doesn't know.

**The voice.** A tired, fair officer with a dry wit: plain words, an investigator's patience, kindness kept for the people who need it. A lawman's last word.

**Never.** What happened at the Shady Rest (later quests), Reethe found, the admiral named (the game's name for him is only in the text), Theramore's lady, the deserters' fate.

**French.** Vouvoie. Vimes is "Vimaire" in the French client.

**The game's.** Smiling Jim and his inn burnt, Reethe's badge, never good news, Tesoran's kind word for traitor, the deserters loyal to the old admiral calling Theramore the traitors and the irony of it, Lost Point, Reethe too reckless for the deserters, Balos's "forget you bumped into us", Caldwell sent, the marsh not so big and trackless (1282 to 1287). **Ours.** Talking with Jim, letting him speak of the inn as standing, granting the irony and nothing else, asking the player not to forget.

## Letter

$N,

I sent Caldwell out to Lost Point, as I said I would. The marsh is not so big and trackless that a man can hide in it forever, though Reethe seems set on testing that.

Jim still smiles at everyone. I stop and talk with him when I can. He speaks of the inn as though it were still standing out on the road, and I let him.

It's never good news when a guard's badge is brought to me. Reethe's was the worst kind: a man so loyal to the old admiral that he turned his back on his oath, then too reckless even for the deserters he ran with. They call us the traitors. I'll grant them the irony.

Balos Jacken asked you to forget you ever bumped into them. Please don't.

Captain Garran Vimes
Theramore
