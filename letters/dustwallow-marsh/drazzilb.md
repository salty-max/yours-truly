# Draz'Zilb

**Who.** Draz'Zilb (4501), an ogre of the Stonemaul clan in Brackenwall Village, Dustwallow Marsh, and an eloquent one. The chain (1169 to 1173): "Whilst that craven fool, Mok'Morokk, wallows in power and Tharg wrestles the demons of sorrow and vengeance and vies for leadership of the clan, I seem to be the only one concerned with identifying the source of aggression on our old home. Why the puzzled stare, $n? Expecting me to speak like an uncouth ruffian merely because I am an ogre?" (1169); the brood of Onyxia that invaded Stonemaul Village (1170); Mok'Morokk: "Me no like dragons. Me like it here. No dragons here" (1170); "Mok'Morokk displayed strength when Rexxar was our leader. But soon after Rexxar's departure Mok'Morokk fell prey to temptations of power" (1171); Onyxia's eggs destroyed at the Wyrmbog, "It will be sometime before Onyxia can produce more eggs... Tharg would make an excellent leader but is hindered by his injuries and age. Drive Mok'Morokk from this place by force" (1172). The anchor (1173): "That cowardly fat mass Mok'Morokk fled like a scared child. You have done a great service, $N. Now my people can appoint a new leader, one who will help us reclaim our old home once and for all." Funny: the coward fled.

**The voice.** A pompous, learned ogre: long words, longer sentences, archaic turns ("'Twas", "whilst"), proud of his diction and dry about his clan. The joke is the gap between him and every other ogre, and he knows it.

**Never.** A new leader named (the game keeps Mok'Morokk in the village: the clan is still deciding), Mok'Morokk gone for good, Onyxia beaten, Rexxar's whereabouts, the third-party laugh at the clan's faces.

**French.** Vouvoie, a precious and slightly old-fashioned French.

**The game's.** Mok'Morokk the craven fool and his flight like a scared child, Tharg too injured and old, Rexxar's departure, Onyxia's brood at Stonemaul, the eggs, being the only one concerned, not an uncouth ruffian (1169 to 1173). **Ours.** Long sentences as genius or illness, ogres deliberating like a rockslide, Mok'Morokk crept back to sulk and nobody forgetting it (the game keeps him in the village), time bought and spent wisely, an eccentric in his own village.

## Letter

$N,

You will forgive the length of this letter. Among my people a long sentence is taken either for a sign of genius or for a symptom of illness, and I have elected to be thought a genius.

Mok'Morokk fled like a frightened child, and though he has since crept back to sulk among us, nobody in Brackenwall has forgotten it. The clan now deliberates upon his successor. Ogres deliberate in the manner of a rockslide: slowly, loudly, and with injuries. Tharg would serve admirably, were he younger and less broken.

Meanwhile Onyxia's brood remains to the south, and I remain the only one in Brackenwall concerned with why. Your work among the eggs has bought us time. I intend to spend it wisely, which in this village makes me something of an eccentric.

With profound regard,
Draz'Zilb
