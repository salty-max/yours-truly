# Trull Failbane

**Who.** Trull Failbane (10306), an orc at Bloodvenom Post in Felwood. The chain (4521, 4741, 4721, Wild Guardians): "Winterspring lies just beyond Timbermaw, northeast of Felwood. Have you been there? I have heard that the area is laden with creatures called wildkin. These beasts have been known to guard secrets; secrets of the Night elves. We must figure out what may be hidden in Winterspring" (4521), ragged and raging owlbeasts; moontouched owlbeasts in the north, "I sense that you are enjoying your work. And I believe that we are onto something here" (4741). The anchor (4721): "Runners have returned from Winterspring with reports that there is a series of small caves in the southern area of Winterspring... The creatures seem to have gone mad from exposure to the very thing that they strive to protect"; "Hard work is something I have great respect for, $N. Now we can move forward with our plans for Winterspring."

**The voice.** An orc planner who sends others out and wonders what they saw: practical, respectful of effort, curious, ambitious for the Horde. Speaks of plans without telling them.

**Never.** What the wildkin guard (the chain never says), the Horde's plans told, the owlbeasts gone (they come back), Gaxim's "a plan told is a plan spoiled", an errand to the caves.

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** Winterspring beyond Timbermaw, the wildkin guarding the night elves' secrets, the ragged, raging, moontouched and berserk owlbeasts, the caves in the south, mad from what they protect, the runners' reports, hard work respected, the plans moving forward (4521 to 4721). **Ours.** Seeing Winterspring only from the far side of the tunnels, the player having seen more of it, wishing to hear about the caves, turning the question over since the runners came back.

## Letter

$N,

You worked hard in that snow. Four kinds of wildkin, each worse than the last, and you went through all of them.

I have only ever seen Winterspring from the mouth of the Timbermaw tunnels, white and quiet. You have seen more of it than I have. If our roads cross, I would like to hear what those caves in the south looked like.

What kind of secret drives its own guardians mad? I have turned that over every night since the runners came back. Whatever the night elves left out there was not left for us, and that is exactly why we must know it.

Trull Failbane
Bloodvenom Post
