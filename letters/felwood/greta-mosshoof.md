# Greta Mosshoof

**Who.** Greta Mosshoof (10922), a tauren druid of the Emerald Circle at the Emerald Sanctuary in Felwood, a woman. The chain (5155 to 5242): "I must know that you are an enemy of the Shadow Council--the ones responsible for many evils here and abroad... To the west you will see a path leading to Jaedenar. It is there that the Council plots with the Burning Legion" (5155); the braziers of Shadow Hold, "If I am to find out any more information about our enemy through scrying, then those braziers must be extinguished" (5157); the corrupt Moon Well water to her old friend Islen Waterseer, who has the spirits of water purify it: "Please give my regards to Greta and my best wishes. She is tackling something greater than many of us... as I'm sure she will soon find out. The Shadow Council is not a force to be trifled with" (5158, 5159); the braziers doused, "I was able to see you with my spells as soon as the last brazier's flames died. Well done, $N" (5165). The anchor (5242): Shadow Lord Fel'dan, "the mortal in power down below... he is the head of the serpent", and his two succubus servants; "Earthmother be praised, $N! Thank you! This is a great day, indeed! The Shadow Council will be hard pressed to scale to the same levels of power they once held with this blow to their hierarchy."

**The voice.** A druid and a scryer: calm, deep, earnest, warm in the Earthmother's name, sober about Felwood's sickness. She saw the player through her spells, and says so.

**Never.** The Shadow Council beaten or Fel'dan gone for good (the head of a serpent grows back: the game brings him back), Felwood healed, Islen's warning answered (it stands), Neeru's Burning Blade (another chain, the same Council).

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** The Shadow Council and the Legion in Jaedenar, the braziers of Shadow Hold, the scrying, seeing the player as the last brazier died, Islen Waterseer, the water purified, her regards and her warning, Fel'dan the head of the serpent, Earthmother be praised, the Council hard pressed (5155 to 5242). **Ours.** The first thing her spells showed, never so glad to see one, the warning she keeps turning over, a serpent with more than one head, one dark corner less sick, the Earthmother seeing it.

## Letter

$N,

I watched you, you know. When the last brazier went dark in Shadow Hold, my spells could see again, and the first thing they showed me was you, standing in the smoke. I have scried a great many things in my life. I do not think I have ever been so glad to see one.

Islen sent her regards with the water, and a warning I have not stopped turning over: that what I am tackling is larger than I know, and that I will find out soon enough. I have never known her to be wrong. The Council was struck at its head in Jaedenar, but a serpent like that has more than one.

Felwood is still sick, and it will be sick for some time yet. But it is a little less sick in one dark corner, and the Earthmother sees that, even where no one else does.

Earthmother keep you,
Greta Mosshoof
Emerald Sanctuary
