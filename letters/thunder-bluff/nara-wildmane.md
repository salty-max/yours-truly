# Nara Wildmane

**Who.** Nara Wildmane (5770), a young tauren druid on the Elder Rise in Thunder Bluff, a woman. The chain (870 to 914): Tonga Runetotem's oases, the fissure at the Forgotten Pools, dead seeds brought to life at the Stagnant Oasis, the snapjaws' tainted shells, "It is as if the land dreams and cannot awake" (870 to 1489); Arch Druid Hamuul Runetotem's dream of a cult of druids in the Wailing Caverns, "I am old and my strength fails... But Nara is young and her spirit is fierce. She remained in the dreaming when I fled" (1490); Nara: "I have seen their twisted, scaled faces. I know their crimes, and I know their plans." The anchor, Leaders of the Fang (914): the Druids of the Fang, "part of an order of noble druids whose plan was to heal the Barrens, but now seek to remake that land to match their own, twisted dreams", four leaders, four dream gems, "Even now their faces haunt me!" And: "My dreams are now free of their wicked faces, and you have helped save the Barrens from a cursed future. I thank you, $N. I thank you for myself, for the druids of Thunder Bluff, and for the land." The plan named Hamuul; Nara is the one the chain changed (her nightmares end) and the one who ends it.

**The voice.** Young, fierce, vivid: dreams told in the body's terms, sleep and waking. Honest about what the dreaming cost. Not a ceremony (Mull's), not a coach (Ventaari's): a survivor of a nightmare, still a little angry.

**Never.** Naralex or the Disciple (the Caverns' other chain, not required), the Druids of the Fang said gone (the leaders return, only her dreams are free), the land said healed, Hamuul's words given to her, Thunder Bluff named (its French name differs between the clients).

**French.** Vouvoie.

**The game's.** The elder fleeing the dreaming and her staying, the faces, the noble order meant to heal the Barrens, the twisted dreams, the four leaders and their gems, dreams free of their faces, the land that dreams and cannot wake, the thanks for herself, the druids and the land (870 to 914). **Ours.** A whole night slept, the first light on the Rise, waking with her hands open and once with her fists closed, the faces turning to her, easier to hate them if they had always been so.

## Letter

$N,

I slept through the night. All of it, from dark to the first light on the Rise, and I woke with my hands open. I had forgotten that sleep could be so plain.

When the elder left the dreaming, I stayed, because someone had to see their faces. I did. Then I could not stop seeing them. Four faces, every night, turning toward me as if they knew me. I used to wake with my fists closed and my teeth aching. They do not come any more.

They were druids once. Their order meant to heal the Barrens. I think about that more than I would like. It would be easier to hate them if they had always been what they became.

The land still dreams, and badly. That was never one night's work. But my dreams belong to me again, and you are the one who took them back.

Nara Wildmane
